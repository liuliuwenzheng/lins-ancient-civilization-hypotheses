## Notes

Primary published report of 1957–58 Kot Diji excavations (Khairpur, Sindh). Distinguishes Mature Harappan upper levels from earlier Kot Dijian / Early Harappan deposits — a key Early Harappan type-site in the Indus valley. Bibliographic details verified via Ancient Pakistan review and Pakistan Archaeology No. 2 digitization (accessed 2026-10-11). No invented layer counts beyond cited report framing.
