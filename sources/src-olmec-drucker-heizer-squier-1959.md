## Notes

Primary BAE final report on the 1955 La Venta excavations (Complex A stratigraphy, offerings, radiocarbon samples). Authors placed major Olmec occupation roughly 800–400 BCE on then-available dates — later recalibrations and San Lorenzo work revise absolute endpoints (see Pool 2007; Inomata et al. 2013 discussion). No invented page numbers.
