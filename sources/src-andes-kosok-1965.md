## Notes

Secondary survey monograph from 1940s fieldwork; recognized dense stone platform-mound clusters in the north-central coast (sometimes labeled “Pativilca Complex”) and noted virtual absence of potsherds at Chupa Cigarro Grande (later Caral), complicating ceramic relative dating. Research-history / site-recognition source—not a modern excavation report. Tier S. Bibliographic details verified via American Antiquity review notice (accessed 2026-10-11). No invented measurements.
