## Notes

Use for Late Archaic maize evidence (pollen, residues, coprolites), inland Fortaleza site descriptions (Caballete, Huaricanga), and diet notes (maize starch dominant among human coprolites in their sample; sweet potato; anchovies as protein). Do not invent quantitative percentages beyond what is needed; prefer qualitative claims when page-level tables are not excerpted. Maize-as-staple conclusion is the authors’ interpretation.
