## Notes

Peer-reviewed field report on 2020– pits 3–8 and site overview. Seed uses stated site size (~12 km²), occupation span (c. 2700–1000 BC), discovery/excavation history, 1986 pits artefact count (via Sichuan 1999), and Pit 4 radiocarbon range. Broader pit-phase absolute chronology is explicitly noted by the authors as long debated — treat as research status, not settled fact.
