## Notes

Peer-reviewed PNAS article proposing that earthquakes, El Niño flooding, beach-ridge formation, and sand-dune incursion ~3,800–3,600 cal B.P. contributed to abandonment of early Supe settlements after a Late Preceramic florescence ~5,800–3,600 cal B.P. Environmental-causation / abrupt-demise framing is a scholarly hypothesis—keep in hypothesis column; do not treat as settled single-cause fact. Tier S.
