## Notes

Primary dissertation on Bandurria (Huaura coast): household excavations (5×5 m units), domestic vs monumental sectors, AMS radiocarbon program complementing Rosa Fung’s earlier dates; challenges models that characterize Late Preceramic society simply as complex chiefdom or state. Complementary household dataset at CADB (cadb.pitt.edu/chu/). Bibliographic details verified via Pitt ETD (accessed 2026-10-11). No invented coordinates or forged images.
