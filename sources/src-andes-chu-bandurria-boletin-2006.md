## Notes

Peer-reviewed article documenting previously unreported monumental architecture at Bandurria (Huaura coast): sector ~20 ha with 10 structures (4 main mounds 8–12 m high; 6 minor). Excavation of Mound 1 revealed cobble-and-mud-mortar truncated pyramid associated with circular sunken plaza, paralleling other Late Preceramic monumental forms. DOI and pagination verified via PUCP journal / CONCYTEC metadata (accessed 2026-10-11). No invented coordinates.
