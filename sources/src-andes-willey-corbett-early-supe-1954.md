## Notes

Primary Institute of Andean Research Project 3 excavation monograph (1941–42 fieldwork) including Supe-mouth work at Aspero (then framed as Early Supe / Chavín Horizon). Historical P-tier: absolute chronologies and ceramic-horizon labels are outdated relative to modern Late Archaic radiocarbon frameworks (see Moseley & Willey 1973 reexamination recognizing artificial platform mounds). Use for early excavation documentation, not current absolute dating. Bibliographic details verified via Columbia UP / American Antiquity review notices (accessed 2026-10-11). No invented page-level artifact claims.
