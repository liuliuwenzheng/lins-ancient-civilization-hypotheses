Primary edited field-report volume for eleven seasons of French Archaeological Mission excavations at Mehrgarh (Kachi District, Balochistan). Covers Neolithic through Indus-related sequences foundational to Early Food Producing / Regionalization frames. Bibliographic details verified via Sudoc/eHRAF listings 2026-10-11. No invented radiocarbon bands beyond what registered secondary chronologies already summarize.
