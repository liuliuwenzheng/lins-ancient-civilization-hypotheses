## Notes

Peer-reviewed report of Late Preclassic painted hieroglyphic text at San Bartolo (Petén), with radiocarbon context indicating writing by the 3rd century BCE (and mural program of the same general horizon). Establishes early lowland Maya script practice before Early Classic dynastic monuments. No invented glyph readings beyond the article’s explicit claims.
