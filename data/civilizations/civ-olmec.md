# 奥尔梅克 / Olmec

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

本条综述中美洲形成期（Formative / Preclassic）海湾低地的奥尔梅克考古文化。Christopher A. Pool（2007）《奥尔梅克考古与早期中美洲》将考古文化置于墨西哥南韦拉克鲁斯与塔巴斯科热带低地：约 3500 年前兴起，其后约千年繁荣；奥尔梅克统治者以中美洲**最早的纪念性石雕艺术**之一表达权力，并以远距离获取的财富作大规模埋藏供奉 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。Richard A. Diehl（2004）通论按「圣洛伦佐领地 → 拉文塔领地 → 日常生活与艺术 → 海湾以外 → 后奥尔梅克（Epi-Olmec）」编排；书名 “America’s First Civilization” 为出版定位，**不**作为本仓事实栏的「最早文明」排序 [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]。一手发掘文献包括：Coe & Diehl（1980）耶鲁大学圣洛伦佐·特诺奇蒂特兰两卷本；Drucker（1952）与 Drucker、Heizer & Squier（1959）史密森学会拉文塔报告；Grove 编（1987）高地查尔卡钦戈遗址专著 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-drucker-la-venta-1952`][`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`]。Cyphers（2004）提供圣洛伦佐雕塑的后期 UNAM 编目综合；Benson & de la Fuente 编（1996）展览目录含 González Lauck「拉文塔：奥尔梅克都城」专章；Clark & Pye 编（2000）国家美术馆研讨会文集覆盖艺术与考古 [`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]。Blomster、Neff & Glascock（2005）以元素分析论证部分形成期陶器自圣洛伦佐地区外销——**互动证据模型，非帝国叙事** [`src-olmec-blomster-neff-glascock-2005`]。Taube（2004）Dumbarton Oaks 藏品目录支持玉石等便携雕塑的物质文化取向 [`src-olmec-taube-doaks-2004`]。Nichols & Pool（2012）牛津手册提供区域通论；与早期玛雅关系见 Inomata 等（2013）——反对拉文塔单向扩散定论 [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`][`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]。

This entry summarizes the Formative Gulf Coast Olmec archaeological culture of southern Veracruz and Tabasco. Pool (2007) places the culture in those tropical lowlands, emerging some 3500 years ago and flourishing over the next millennium, with Mesoamerica’s first monumental stone art and massive buried offerings of distant wealth [`src-olmec-pool-2007`]. Diehl (2004) surveys San Lorenzo, La Venta, daily life/art, Olmecs abroad, and Epi-Olmec — subtitle primacy is book framing, **not** a fact-library ranking [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]. Primary reports include Coe & Diehl (1980) on San Lorenzo; Drucker (1952) and Drucker, Heizer & Squier (1959) on La Venta; Grove ed. (1987) on Chalcatzingo [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-drucker-la-venta-1952`][`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`]. Cyphers (2004), Benson & de la Fuente eds. (1996; González Lauck on La Venta), and Clark & Pye eds. (2000) update sculpture, capital framing, and regional art/archaeology [`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]. Blomster et al. (2005) argue elemental evidence for San Lorenzo–region pottery export — **interaction model, not empire narrative** [`src-olmec-blomster-neff-glascock-2005`]. Taube (2004) orients portable greenstone sculpture [`src-olmec-taube-doaks-2004`]. Nichols & Pool (2012) and Inomata et al. (2013) frame Mesoamerican context and reject one-way La Venta → Maya diffusion as settled fact [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`][`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`].

## Geography & environment

奥尔梅克心脏地带（常称 Olman）位于墨西哥湾沿岸南部低地：今南韦拉克鲁斯与塔巴斯科的热带河流冲积与湿地环境 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]。圣洛伦佐坐落于夸察夸尔科斯（Coatzacoalcos）河系抬升台地/岛状地形；拉文塔位于塔巴斯科通杜普拉（Tonalá）河附近的岛状高地，周围为沼泽与冲积平原 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`]。Coe & Diehl 第二卷从河畔民族生态学讨论资源与生计背景 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`]。形成期奥尔梅克风格的纪念性艺术与器物亦出现在海湾以外的高地与太平洋沿岸等互动区（如莫雷洛斯的查尔卡钦戈）——地理上超出「心脏地带」不等于已证明的殖民帝国 [`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]。

The Olmec heartland (often called Olman) lies in the southern Gulf lowlands of southern Veracruz and Tabasco — tropical riverine and wetland settings [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]. San Lorenzo occupies elevated terrain in the Coatzacoalcos drainage; La Venta sits on an island-like rise near the Tonalá River amid swamps and alluvium [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`]. Coe & Diehl Volume 2 treats riverine ethnoecology [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`]. Formative Olmec-style monuments and objects also appear outside the Gulf heartland (e.g. Chalcatzingo in Morelos) — extra-heartland distribution is not proof of a colonial empire [`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`].

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

海湾奥尔梅克中心以土筑平台、广场、排水/池塘工程与巨型石雕的空间配置为特征；Pool 与 Diehl 提供心脏地带制度与景观的二手综合 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]。圣洛伦佐的台地改造、雕塑安置与定居布局见 Coe & Diehl 与 Cyphers [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`]。拉文塔 Complex A 的仪式建筑序列、玉器等埋藏供奉与土墩/金字塔规划见 1955 年发掘报告及后续综合 [`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`]。纪念性石雕（巨石头像、祭坛/王座、石碑等）被 Pool 表述为中美洲最早的纪念性石雕艺术传统之一——**「最早」相对于中美洲考古记录的形成期语境，非全球排名** [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`]。统治者权力的物质表达（石雕、远距离财富埋藏）是通论主题；具体「国家」定义仍属开放模型讨论（见 Notes）[`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]。

Gulf Olmec centers feature earthen platforms, plazas, hydraulic works, and spatial programs of colossal stone monuments; Pool and Diehl synthesize heartland institutions and landscapes [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]. San Lorenzo’s plateau modification, monument placement, and settlement layout: Coe & Diehl and Cyphers [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`]. La Venta Complex A ritual architecture, buried offerings, and mound planning: 1955 report and later syntheses [`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-benson-fuente-1996`]. Monumental stone sculpture (colossal heads, altars/thrones, stelae) is framed by Pool among Mesoamerica’s first monumental stone-art traditions — **“first” within Formative Mesoamerican archaeology, not a global ranking** [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`]. Elite power via monuments and distant wealth offerings is a survey theme; “state” definitions remain open models (Notes) [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`].

## Writing, language & administration

形成期海湾奥尔梅克**没有**类似迈锡尼 Linear B 或商代甲骨那样被普遍接受的长篇行政文字档案。纪念性石雕上的符号/图像程序是图像学与早期文字讨论的对象；后奥尔梅克（Epi-Olmec）阶段的长铭文传统（如特雷斯·萨波特斯等相关讨论）落在 Diehl 专章取向之外的本条范围边缘——**本条不宣称已破译的奥尔梅克「文字系统」** [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]。行政与仪式组织的证据目前主要来自建筑规划、供奉埋藏、雕塑安置与远距离交换，而非泥板式簿记 [`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。

Formative Gulf Olmec lacks a generally accepted long administrative script archive comparable to Linear B or Shang oracle bones. Signs/imagery on monuments are topics in iconography and early-writing debates; longer Epi-Olmec inscriptions lie at the edge of this entry’s scope via Diehl’s TOC only — **this entry asserts no deciphered Olmec “writing system”** [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]. Evidence for organization comes mainly from architectural planning, offerings, monument placement, and long-distance exchange rather than tablet bookkeeping [`src-olmec-drucker-heizer-squier-1959`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`].

## Economy, subsistence & craft

海湾低地生计与河畔/湿地资源、玉米等农业及区域交换相关；Coe & Diehl 第二卷提供圣洛伦佐河畔民族生态背景 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。远距离获取的绿色岩、黑曜石、玉料等用于雕塑、斧锛与供奉，是 Pool / Diehl 通论中的财富与仪式经济主题 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-taube-doaks-2004`]。Blomster 等（2005）INAA 研究主张部分早期形成期雕刻灰陶等器物自圣洛伦佐地区生产并出现在外地遗址——支持海湾中心参与外销网络；该结论在学界有后续讨论/批评，**登记为有出处的互动模型** [`src-olmec-blomster-neff-glascock-2005`]。巨型玄武岩头像等石材需从图斯特拉山脉等来源搬运——工程与劳动动员是通论议题，具体路线与劳动力数字须跟进专文，本条不编造 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。

Gulf lowland subsistence relates to riverine/wetland resources, maize agriculture, and regional exchange; Coe & Diehl Volume 2 supplies San Lorenzo ethnoecological background [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]. Long-distance greenstone, obsidian, and jade for sculpture, celts, and offerings are wealth/ritual-economy themes in Pool/Diehl [`src-olmec-pool-2007`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-taube-doaks-2004`]. Blomster et al. (2005) argue INAA evidence that some Early Formative carved gray wares were produced in the San Lorenzo region and found abroad — a sourced export-network model with later debate [`src-olmec-blomster-neff-glascock-2005`]. Colossal basalt heads imply transport from Tuxtla-area sources — labor mobilization is a survey theme; no invented tonnage or workforce figures [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`][`src-olmec-cyphers-escultura-2004`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`].

## External contacts

Formative Mesoamerica shows interaction between the Gulf heartland and highland/Pacific zones; Chalcatzingo is a classic extra-heartland anchor [`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`][`src-olmec-diehl-2004`][`src-olmec-clark-pye-nga-2000`]. ## Notes

- **Scope:** Gulf Coast Formative Olmec heartland (San Lorenzo, La Venta) plus selected interaction evidence (Chalcatzingo; pottery-export debates). Not a full catalogue of every Olmec-style monument in Mesoamerica.
- **Open debate — mother culture vs sister cultures:** Whether Gulf Olmec were a singular “mother culture” or peers in multi-directional Formative networks remains contested. Inomata et al. (2013) argue against one-way La Venta → Maya diffusion for early Middle Preclassic ceremonial architecture [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`].
- **Not claimed:** global or hemispheric “first civilization” ranking (reject Diehl title language as fact).
- **Blomster et al. 2005:** pottery-export INAA results are registered interaction evidence; published critiques/rejoinders exist — do not upgrade to “Olmec empire” narrative.
- **Absolute chronology:** Pool’s ~3500 ya / ~1000-year floruit and conventional ca. 1500–400 BCE labels are approximate; San Lorenzo vs La Venta endpoints continue to be refined.
- **Epi-Olmec:** noted via Diehl TOC only; not expanded here.
- **Density D-27:** structured pack expanded (sources 13→28; sites 3→12). Mother culture, empire, San Lorenzo-as-sole-capital, fully deciphered writing, unitary were-jaguar religion, unidirectional pottery export, Manatí=state ritual, Stela C Long Count origin = hypotheses.
