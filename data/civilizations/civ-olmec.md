# 奥尔梅克 / Olmec

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述中美洲形成期（Formative / Preclassic）海湾低地的奥尔梅克考古文化。Christopher A. Pool（2007）《奥尔梅克考古与早期中美洲》剑桥专著摘要写明：该考古文化出现于墨西哥南韦拉克鲁斯与塔巴斯科热带低地，约 3500 年前兴起，其后约千年繁荣；奥尔梅克统治者以中美洲**最早的纪念性石雕艺术**之一表达物质与宗教权力，并以远距离获取的财富作大规模埋藏供奉；形成期早、中段亦为玛雅等中美洲文明奠基时段的学术叙述框架 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。Richard A. Diehl（2004）《奥尔梅克》通论按「圣洛伦佐领地 → 拉文塔领地 → 日常生活与艺术 → 海湾以外的东部/西部中美洲 → 后奥尔梅克（Epi-Olmec）」编排，是海湾心脏地带双中心结构的二手锚点；书名中的 “America’s First Civilization” 为出版定位用语，**不**作为本仓事实栏的世界/美洲「最早文明」排序 [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]。Michael D. Coe 与 Richard A. Diehl（1980）《奥尔梅克之地》两卷本是耶鲁大学圣洛伦佐·特诺奇蒂特兰（San Lorenzo Tenochtitlán）发掘的历史一手出版（第 1 卷遗址考古；第 2 卷河畔民族生态），登记为海湾早期形成期核心遗址的 P 档报告 [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`]。David C. Grove 主编（1987）《古代查尔卡钦戈》记录墨西哥莫雷洛斯高地查尔卡钦戈遗址——海湾心脏地带以外、带奥尔梅克风格纪念性艺术的形成期中心——作为互动/同侪网络证据，而非「殖民地」定论 [`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`]。Nichols & Pool 编（2012）牛津《中美洲考古手册》提供区域取向的 S 档通论框架 [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]。与早期玛雅的关系：Inomata 等（2013）以塞瓦尔（Ceibal）测年论证低地玛雅早期仪式建筑复合体**并非**单纯由拉文塔单向扩散而来，而涉及多区域互动；「母文化」模型仍是开放争议（见 Notes） [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]。

This seed summarizes the Formative Gulf Coast Olmec archaeological culture of southern Veracruz and Tabasco. Pool’s 2007 Cambridge monograph abstract places the Olmec in those tropical lowlands, emerging some 3500 years ago and flourishing over the next millennium; it credits Olmec rulers with Mesoamerica’s first monumental stone art and with massive buried offerings of wealth obtained from great distances, and frames early/middle Formative foundations for later Mesoamerican civilizations including the Maya [`src-olmec-pool-2007`]. Diehl’s 2004 survey is organized around San Lorenzo’s realm, La Venta’s realm, daily life/art, Olmecs abroad (eastern and western Mesoamerica), and Epi-Olmec culture — a secondary heartland map; the subtitle “America’s First Civilization” is book framing, **not** a fact-library primacy ranking [`src-olmec-diehl-2004`]. Coe & Diehl (1980) *In the Land of the Olmec* is the two-volume primary publication of the Yale San Lorenzo Tenochtitlán project (Vol. 1 site archaeology; Vol. 2 riverine ethnoecology) [`src-olmec-coe-diehl-san-lorenzo-1980`]. Grove ed. (1987) *Ancient Chalcatzingo* documents a highland Morelos Formative center with Olmec-style monumental art outside the Gulf heartland — an interaction/peer-network anchor, not a colony verdict [`src-olmec-grove-chalcatzingo-1987`]. Nichols & Pool eds. (2012) supply a broad Mesoamerican handbook orientation [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]. On Early Maya relations, Inomata et al. (2013) argue from Ceibal that early lowland plaza-pyramid development was not one-directional diffusion from La Venta but interregional interaction; mother-culture models remain debated (see Notes) [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`].

## Notes

- library-seed: cited overview; expand later.
- **Scope:** Gulf Coast Formative Olmec heartland (San Lorenzo, La Venta) plus selected interaction evidence (e.g. Chalcatzingo). Not a full catalogue of every Olmec-style monument in Mesoamerica.
- **Open scholarly debate (not settled fact) — mother culture vs sister cultures:** Whether the Gulf Olmec were a singular “mother culture” that generated later Mesoamerican civilizations, or participated as peers / “sister cultures” in multi-directional Formative networks, remains contested. Inomata et al. (2013) explicitly contrast mother-culture models with limited-influence / competing-peer models and argue against one-way La Venta → Maya diffusion for early Middle Preclassic ceremonial architecture [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]. Keep preferred model language in NOTES or `hypotheses/`.
- **Not claimed:** global or hemispheric “first civilization” ranking (reject Diehl title language as fact).
- **Absolute chronology:** Pool’s “~3500 years ago / next 1000 years” and conventional ca. 1500–400 BCE labels are approximate; San Lorenzo vs La Venta absolute endpoints continue to be refined (Inomata et al. 2013 discuss Bayesian reassessments). Re-check when expanding.
- **Epi-Olmec:** noted via Diehl TOC only; not expanded in this seed.
