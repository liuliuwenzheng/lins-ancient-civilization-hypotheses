# 中国青铜时代（中原总览） / China Bronze Age (Central Plains overview)

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

本条综述中国青铜时代在**中原学术常规框架**下的可核验事实：以二里头（Erlitou）考古文化、二里岗（Erligang）期、殷墟/安阳（Anyang / Yinxu）晚商与商–周青铜礼器传统为中心，**不**把「夏王朝」写成已定论历史事实。Robert L. Thorp（1991）整理二里头类型遗址已发表材料，并提出对考古数据的「最小」定义——**不预设夏的历史真实性** [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]。Li Liu 与 Xingcan Chen（2003）讨论约公元前 1800–1400 年早期国家形成中金属与盐等关键外部资源获取的动力学 [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]。同一作者对（2012）剑桥《中国考古学》将中原早期国家形成专章标为二里头与二里岗（约公元前 1900/1800–1250 年），晚商与邻境专章标为约公元前 1250–1046 年，并在全书层面强调社会政治演化的多中心性 [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`]。Roderick B. Campbell（2014）按二里头期 → 二里岗期 → 小双桥–洹北期 → 安阳期综述第二千纪中原大都市及其更广互动带 [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]。Robert W. Bagley（1987）Sackler 藏商代礼器青铜专书，是英语学界讨论中原商代青铜礼器技术与器类的标准锚点；其在 Loewe & Shaughnessy（1999）中的「商考古学」专章区分文本「商」与更广的考古证据 [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`][`src-china-loewe-shaughnessy-cambridge-1999`]。David N. Keightley（1978）系统介绍晚商甲骨文作为史料 [`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]。K. C. Chang（1980）与 Thorp（2006）提供商文明通论；Li Chi（1977）记述安阳发掘史 [`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`]。Xu Hong 在 Underhill（2013）companion 中专章综述二里头文化 [`src-china-underhill-companion-2013`]。Rowan K. Flad 与 Pochan Chen（2013）综论长江上游与中游（含成都平原）自晚新石器时代至青铜时代结束，用以标明青铜时代中国**不止中原一条线** [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]。西南四川盆地的三星堆另立 `civ-sanxingdui`，本条不把它写成商王国的同一政体。

This entry summarizes a **Central Plains scholarly conventional frame** for China’s Bronze Age—centered on the Erlitou archaeological culture, Erligang, Anyang/Yinxu Late Shang, and Shang–Zhou ritual bronze traditions—without treating the Xia dynasty as settled historical fact. Thorp (1991) reviews published Erlitou typesite data and proposes a “minimal” archaeological definition that **does not assume Xia historicity** [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]. Liu & Chen (2003) examine early Chinese state formation with a publisher-stated focus on metal and salt procurement in ~1800–1400 BCE [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]. Their 2012 Cambridge synthesis titles Central Plains early-state formation as Erlitou and Erligang (~1900/1800–1250 BC) and Late Shang and neighbors (~1250–1046 BC), stressing multicentric sociopolitical evolution [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`]. Campbell (2014) surveys second-millennium metropolitan centers through Erlitou → Erligang → Xiaoshuangqiao–Huanbei → Anyang periods within wider interaction zones [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]. Bagley’s 1987 Sackler catalogue anchors Shang ritual bronze technology and vessel types; his “Shang Archaeology” chapter in Loewe & Shaughnessy (1999) distinguishes textual Shang from broader archaeological evidence [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`][`src-china-loewe-shaughnessy-cambridge-1999`]. Keightley (1978) introduces late Shang oracle bones as historical sources [`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]. Chang (1980) and Thorp (2006) supply Shang civilization syntheses; Li Chi (1977) narrates Anyang excavation history [`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`]. Xu Hong’s companion chapter reviews the Erlitou culture [`src-china-underhill-companion-2013`]. Flad & Chen (2013) synthesize upper/middle Yangzi archaeology (including the Chengdu Plain) from Late Neolithic through the end of the Bronze Age, marking that Bronze Age China is **not only** a Central Plains story [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]. Sanxingdui is registered separately as `civ-sanxingdui` and is **not** asserted here as the same polity as the Shang kingdom.

## Geography & environment

「中原 / Central Plains」在本条指黄河中下游冲积平原及相关黄土台地—河谷地带，作为二里头、二里岗与殷墟等中心讨论的地理核心；北部边疆、长江流域与四川盆地等属更广互动带或区域青铜传统，须另有出处支撑 [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`][`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]。Liu & Chen（2003）强调早期国家形成动力与**金属、盐**等关键外部资源获取相关——暗示中原中心对腹地以外资源区的依赖，而非封闭自给叙事 [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]。Flad & Chen（2013）将政治、经济与礼仪活动的「中心—边缘」在长江上游与中游（含三峡、湖北、成都平原）呈现为**不必重合**的空间格局 [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]。本条不在未核对定量表格前给出灌区面积、人口或矿产量数字。

“Central Plains” here denotes the middle–lower Yellow River alluvial core and related loess terrace/valley settings that host Erlitou, Erligang, and Yinxu discussions; northern frontiers, the Yangzi, and the Sichuan Basin are wider interaction zones or peer regional traditions and need separate sourcing [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`][`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]. Liu & Chen (2003) link early-state dynamics to procurement of key external resources—especially **metal and salt**—implying dependence on hinterland resource zones rather than closed autarky [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]. Flad & Chen (2013) argue that political, economic, and ritual “centers and peripheries” along the upper/middle Yangzi need not coincide spatially [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]. No irrigated-hectare, population, or ore-yield figures are asserted without checked tables.

## Key sites

- **二里头 / Erlitou（河南偃师）** — 中原早期青铜时代关键类型遗址；Thorp（1991）综述已发表的布局、建筑、墓葬、礼器青铜与玉器等材料，并主张不预设夏史实的最小考古定义 [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]。Xu Hong（Underhill 2013）专章讨论二里头文化的都城布局、青铜生产、墓葬与礼仪 [`src-china-underhill-companion-2013`]。Liu & Chen（2012）将其置于中原早期国家形成叙事的核心节点之一 [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`]。
- **郑州商城及相关二里岗中心 / Zhengzhou and Erligang centers** — Campbell「二里岗期」与 Liu & Chen「二里头与二里岗」框架下的早期都会/中心；城垣与分期细节以《郑州二里岗》1959 与《郑州商城》2001 为准，本条不编造未核对周长数字 [`src-china-zhengzhou-erligang-1959`][`src-china-zhengzhou-shangcheng-2001`][`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]。
- **小双桥、洹北商城 / Xiaoshuangqiao, Huanbei** — Campbell 专章所标的二里岗—安阳过渡期重要地点 [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]。
- **殷墟 / 安阳 / Yinxu, Anyang（河南）** — 晚商都城考古区；甲骨文、王陵区与铸铜手工业等证据类型的核心出土地。Li Chi（1977）记述现代科学发掘开创史；Liu & Chen（2012）晚商专章、Campbell 安阳期章、Chang（1980）与 Thorp（2006）通论提供综合入口 [`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`][`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`][`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`]。

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

中原第二千纪出现以二里头、二里岗与安阳为代表的都会/中心聚落与礼仪—政治建筑传统。Campbell（2014）明确以「都市形成的多样性」为分析对象，并将中原中心放在从长江到蒙古高原边缘、从黄海到青藏高原/甘肃走廊的互动带中理解 [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]。Liu & Chen（2012）强调社会政治演化的多中心与政体间竞争 [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`]。Xu Hong 综述二里头的都城布局与礼仪空间 [`src-china-underhill-companion-2013`]。Thorp（1991）讨论二里头建筑与墓葬材料时坚持考古最小定义 [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]。晚商安阳的宫殿区、宗庙/祭祀与王陵制度，见于 Chang、Thorp 2006 与 Liu & Chen 晚商专章的综合叙述；具体基址编号以正式发掘报告为准，本条不编造 [`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`][`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`]。「国家 / state」在 Liu & Chen、Campbell 等书中是分析概念——**本条将其标记为学术用语，而非对所有第二千纪政体的统一法律定性**。

Second-millennium Central Plains urbanism is exemplified at Erlitou, Erligang, and Anyang centers with ritual–political architecture. Campbell (2014) analyzes the variety of urban formations and situates Central Plains centers within interaction zones from the Yangzi to the Mongolian steppe edge and from the Yellow Sea to the Tibetan plateau / Gansu corridor [`src-china-campbell-bronze-age-2014`]. Liu & Chen (2012) stress multicentric evolution and inter-polity competition [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`]. Xu Hong surveys Erlitou capital layout and ritual space [`src-china-underhill-companion-2013`]. Thorp (1991) keeps a minimal archaeological definition when discussing Erlitou architecture and burials [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]. Late Shang palace, ancestral/ritual, and royal cemetery institutions are oriented via Chang, Thorp 2006, and Liu & Chen’s Late Shang chapter; locus numbers require formal reports — none invented here [`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`][`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`]. “State” in these syntheses is an analytical term — **labeled as scholarly vocabulary, not a uniform legal verdict for every second-millennium polity**.

## Writing, language & administration

晚商安阳期出现大量**甲骨文**（甲骨卜辞）：Keightley（1978）说明其集中于武丁至帝辛约八九位商王时期，契刻于约十万片卜骨与卜甲残片上，并讨论占卜程序、释读、断代与史料价值 [`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]。甲骨文是目前可系统释读的最早中国书写传统之一，记录商王室占卜与相关行政/礼仪议题；更早的二里头/二里岗阶段虽有符号与陶文讨论，但**不等同于**已形成的甲骨文书写系统——本条不把未破译或争议符号写成「已是汉字」[`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]。青铜器铭文（金文）在晚商—西周逐渐重要，Bagley 礼器传统与 Chang/Thorp 通论提供器类与铭文研究入口 [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`][`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`]。语言层面：甲骨文记录的是早期汉语的一种阶段；本条不展开历史语言学专论。

Late Shang Anyang yields abundant **oracle-bone inscriptions**: Keightley (1978) focuses on roughly eight or nine kings from Wu Ding to Di Xin, with divination texts carved on about 100,000 scapula and plastron fragments, covering procedures, decipherment, dating, and historical use [`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]. Oracle-bone script is among the earliest systematically readable Chinese writing traditions, recording royal divination and related ritual/administrative concerns. Earlier Erlitou/Erligang marks are **not equated here** with a fully formed oracle-bone writing system [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-keightley-oracle-bones-1978`]. Bronze inscriptions (jinwen) grow in importance through Late Shang–Western Zhou; Bagley, Chang, and Thorp orient vessel and inscription study [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`][`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`]. Linguistically, oracle bones record an early stage of Chinese; no historical-linguistics monograph is attempted here.

## Economy, subsistence & craft

中原青铜时代经济建立在黄河流域农业（以粟黍等旱作传统为常见讨论焦点，稻作在南方区域更突出）与家畜饲养之上，并与青铜铸造、制陶、制玉、制骨等手工业相连——具体作物组合随遗址与时期而异，须以各遗址植物考古报告为准，本条不编造统一「主粮百分比」[`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`]。Liu & Chen（2003）突出**金属与盐**等外部资源获取对约公元前 1800–1400 年国家形成动力学的推动 [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]。Bagley（1987）详论商代礼器青铜的块范铸造等技术传统 [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`]。安阳等地铸铜作坊与骨器作坊是晚商手工业考古的常见证据类型 [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`]。长江流域的资源与手工业格局见 Flad & Chen（2013）[`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]。

Central Plains Bronze Age economies rest on Yellow River agriculture (millet traditions are a common northern focus; rice is more salient in southern regions) and animal husbandry, linked to bronze casting, ceramics, jade, and bone working — crop mixes vary by site and period and require archaeobotanical reports; no invented staple percentages [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-chang-shang-civilization-1980`]. Liu & Chen (2003) emphasize procurement of **metal and salt** as drivers in the ~1800–1400 BCE state-formation window [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]. Bagley (1987) details Shang ritual-bronze piece-mold casting traditions [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`]. Bronze and bone workshops at Anyang are standard Late Shang craft evidence types [`src-china-liu-chen-archaeology-2012`][`src-china-li-chi-anyang-1977`][`src-china-thorp-early-bronze-age-2006`]. Yangzi resource and craft landscapes: Flad & Chen (2013) [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`].

## Notes

- **Xia dynasty (open debate — not settled fact):** Thorp (1991) explicitly recommends a minimal archaeological definition that does **not** assume Xia historicity. Equating Erlitou phases with a Xia dynasty (or specific textual capitals) remains contested. Keep Xia-as-dynasty claims in NOTES or `hypotheses/` [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`].
- **XSZCP chronology (contested scheme):** The 2000 brief report’s absolute bands (including publicity figures such as Xia ~2070 BCE, Shang ~1600 BCE, Western Zhou start 1046 BCE) are a documented national project scheme with substantial methodological debate. This library cites them as **one labeled scheme**, not unique calendar truth [`src-china-xszcp-jianben-2000`].
- **Scope:** This overview privileges Central Plains Erlitou / Erligang / Anyang bronze traditions. Regional bronze cultures (Sichuan Sanxingdui, middle Yangzi, northern frontiers) are peers or related entries, not automatically “provincial Shang.”
- **Terminology:** Prefer **Erlitou archaeological culture** and **Shang–Zhou bronze traditions** when Xia is unsettled.
- Absolute calendar endpoints remain approximate and calibration-/periodization-dependent; re-check when expanding with formal Chinese excavation reports (Erlitou volumes; Zhengzhou; Yinxu IA CASS reports).
- No invented page numbers; no forged artefact images.
