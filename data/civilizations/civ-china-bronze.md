# 中国青铜时代（中原总览） / China Bronze Age (Central Plains overview)

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述中国青铜时代在**中原学术常规框架**下的种子级事实：以二里头（Erlitou）考古文化与商–周青铜礼器传统为中心，**不**把「夏王朝」写成已定论历史事实。Robert L. Thorp（1991）在《Early China》中整理二里头类型遗址已发表材料（布局、建筑、墓葬、礼器青铜与玉器等），并明确提出对当前考古数据的「最小」定义——**不预设夏的历史真实性** [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]。Li Liu 与 Xingcan Chen（2003）从跨学科角度讨论早期中国国家形成，出版方概述将其焦点放在约公元前 1800–1400 年，并强调金属与盐等关键外部资源获取推动该时期国家形成动力学 [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]。Robert W. Bagley（1987）Sackler 藏商代礼器青铜专书，是英语学界讨论中原商代青铜礼器技术与器类的标准二次文献锚点 [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`]。Rowan K. Flad 与 Pochan Chen（2013）综论长江上游与中游（含成都平原等）自晚新石器时代（约公元前第三千纪晚期）至青铜时代结束（约公元前第一千纪晚期）的考古，用以标明青铜时代中国**不止中原一条线** [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]。西南四川盆地的三星堆另立 `civ-sanxingdui`，本条不把它写成商王国的同一政体。

This seed summarizes a **Central Plains scholarly conventional frame** for China’s Bronze Age—centered on the Erlitou archaeological culture and Shang–Zhou ritual bronze traditions—without treating the Xia dynasty as settled historical fact. Thorp (1991) reviews published Erlitou typesite data (layout, architecture, burials, prestige bronzes and jades) and proposes a “minimal” definition of the archaeological evidence that **does not assume Xia historicity** [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`]. Liu & Chen (2003) examine early Chinese state formation; the publisher’s statement of scope places their resource-procurement thesis (especially metal and salt) in the period ~1800–1400 BCE [`src-china-liu-chen-state-formation-2003`]. Bagley’s 1987 Sackler catalogue is a standard English-language synthesis of Shang ritual bronzes (technology, vessel types, styles) anchoring the Central Plains Shang bronze tradition [`src-china-bagley-shang-ritual-bronzes-1987`]. Flad & Chen (2013) synthesize upper and middle Yangzi archaeology (including the Chengdu Plain) from the Late Neolithic (late third millennium BCE) through the end of the Bronze Age (late first millennium BCE), marking that Bronze Age China is **not only** a Central Plains story [`src-china-flad-chen-ancient-central-china-2013`]. Sanxingdui in the Sichuan Basin is registered separately as `civ-sanxingdui` and is **not** asserted here as the same polity as the Shang kingdom.

## Notes

- library-seed: cited overview; expand later.
- **Xia dynasty (open debate — not settled fact):** Thorp (1991) explicitly recommends a minimal archaeological definition that does **not** assume Xia historicity. Equating Erlitou phases with a Xia dynasty (or specific textual capitals) remains contested across Chinese and overseas scholarship. Keep Xia-as-dynasty claims in NOTES or `hypotheses/` until a dedicated sourced debate entry exists [`src-china-thorp-erlitou-xia-1991`].
- **Scope:** This overview privileges Central Plains Erlitou / Shang–Zhou bronze traditions. Regional bronze cultures (Sichuan Sanxingdui, middle Yangzi, etc.) are peers or related entries, not automatically “provincial Shang.”
- **Terminology:** “Xia–Shang–Zhou” is a conventional historiographic sequence in Chinese scholarship; this seed uses **Erlitou archaeological culture** and **Shang–Zhou bronze traditions** as the safer fact-library labels when Xia is unsettled.
- Absolute calendar endpoints remain approximate and calibration-/periodization-dependent; re-check when expanding with site reports (e.g. Erlitou formal reports, Anyang / Zhengzhou volumes).
