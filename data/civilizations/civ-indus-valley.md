# 印度河 / Indus Valley

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述南亚西北部印度河传统（Indus Valley Tradition / Indus civilisation）的学术常规框架。Jonathan Mark Kenoyer（1991）将印度河传统的主要时段框为：区域化时代（Regionalization Era）约公元前 5000–2600 年；整合时代（Integration Era，哈拉帕阶段 / Mature urban phase）约公元前 2600–1900 年；地方化时代（Localization Era）约公元前 1900–1300 年 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。Rita P. Wright（2010）将印度河文明的繁荣期（florescence）写为约公元前 2600–1900 年，并指出主要中心分布于印度河与加加尔–哈克拉（Ghaggar-Hakra）河系，地理范围可延伸至今日东北阿富汗、巴基斯坦与印度 [`src-indus-wright-2010`]。Kenoyer（2014）剑桥世界史前史专章与 Wright、Possehl 合论：印度河文字（Indus script）仍因缺乏双语文本等原因而**未破译**——此为研究现况，非破译主张 [`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`]。John Marshall 主编的 1931 年官方报告是摩亨佐-达罗（Mohenjo-daro，1922–1927）发掘的一手文献 [`src-indus-marshall-1931`]。Richard H. Meadow 主编的 1991 年卷汇总哈拉帕（Harappa）1986–1990 季现代多学科发掘（Dales / Kenoyer 等） [`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`]。

This seed summarizes the conventional scholarly frame for the Indus Valley Tradition / Indus civilisation in northwestern South Asia. Kenoyer (1991) places the Tradition’s eras as Regionalization ca. 5000–2600 BCE, Integration (Harappan Phase) 2600–1900 BCE, and Localization 1900–1300 BCE [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Wright (2010) states Indus florescence between 2600 and 1900 BCE, with major centers on the Indus and Ghaggar-Hakra systems and an extent from today’s northeast Afghanistan into Pakistan and India [`src-indus-wright-2010`]. Kenoyer’s 2014 Cambridge handbook chapter, with Wright and Possehl, treats the Indus script as still undeciphered (no bilingual texts) — a research-status fact, not a decipherment claim [`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`]. Marshall’s 1931 official account documents the Government of India excavations at Mohenjo-daro (1922–1927) [`src-indus-marshall-1931`]. Meadow’s 1991 edited volume compiles the 1986–1990 multidisciplinary Harappa excavations (Dales / Kenoyer et al.) [`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`].

## Notes

- **Political form (open debate — not settled fact):** Whether the Integration Era is best labeled a territorial “empire,” a network of cities/polities, a “state,” or another form of complex society remains contested in the literature (Kenoyer 1991 reviews state vs chiefdom arguments; Possehl and others emphasize complexity without a Mesopotamia-style palace/temple package). Keep empire/state labels in NOTES or `hypotheses/` until a dedicated sourced debate entry exists [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`].
- **Terminology:** “Indus Valley Civilization,” “Harappan Civilization,” and “Indus Tradition” are overlapping scholarly labels; this seed follows Kenoyer’s Tradition / Integration Era framing without declaring one name exclusive.
- Marshall 1931 absolute chronology is historical; prefer Kenoyer 1991 / Wright 2010 for seed date ranges.
- Early / Mature / Late Harappan popular labels roughly map onto Regionalization → Integration → Localization in Kenoyer’s Table I; endpoints remain approximate and calibration-dependent.
