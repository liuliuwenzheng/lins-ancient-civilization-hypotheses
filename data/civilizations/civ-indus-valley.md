# 印度河 / Indus Valley

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

本条综述南亚西北部印度河传统（Indus Valley Tradition / Indus civilisation / Harappan）的考古—历史框架。Jonathan Mark Kenoyer（1991）采用 Shaffer 的「传统 / 时代 / 阶段」用语，将印度河传统主要时段框为：早期食物生产时代约公元前 6500–5000 年；区域化时代（Regionalization Era）约公元前 5000–2600 年；整合时代（Integration Era，哈拉帕阶段 / Mature urban phase）约公元前 2600–1900 年；地方化时代（Localization Era）约公元前 1900–1300 年 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。Rita P. Wright（2010）将印度河文明繁荣期（florescence）写为约公元前 2600–1900 年，主要中心分布于印度河与加加尔–哈克拉（Ghaggar-Hakra）河系，地理范围可延伸至今日东北阿富汗、巴基斯坦与印度 [`src-indus-wright-2010`]。Kenoyer（1998）通论与 Kenoyer（2014）剑桥世界史前史专章，以及 Possehl（2002）当代综合，提供城市、生计、技术与社会经济组织的二手入口 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1998`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`]。一手发掘文献包括：Marshall 主编 1931 年摩亨佐-达罗（1922–1927）官方报告；Mackay 1938 年续掘报告（1927–1931）；Vats 1940 年哈拉帕早期发掘报告；Meadow 编 1991 年哈拉帕 1986–1990 多学科卷；Rao 1979 年洛塔尔 ASI 专报 [`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-mackay-1938`][`src-indus-vats-1940`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`][`src-indus-rao-lothal-1979`]。印度河文字因缺乏双语文本等原因，在学界仍无普遍接受的破译——此为研究现况，非「永不可破」断言 [`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`]。

This entry summarizes the scholarly frame for the Indus Valley Tradition / Indus civilisation / Harappan in northwestern South Asia. Kenoyer (1991), using Shaffer’s Tradition/Era/Phase terminology, places Early Food Producing ca. 6500–5000 BCE, Regionalization ca. 5000–2600 BCE, Integration (Harappan Phase) 2600–1900 BCE, and Localization 1900–1300 BCE [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Wright (2010) states Indus florescence between 2600 and 1900 BCE, with major centers on the Indus and Ghaggar-Hakra systems and an extent from today’s northeast Afghanistan into Pakistan and India [`src-indus-wright-2010`]. Kenoyer’s 1998 synthesis and 2014 Cambridge handbook chapter, with Possehl’s 2002 overview, orient urbanism, subsistence, technology, and socioeconomic organization [`src-indus-kenoyer-1998`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`]. Primary excavation literature includes Marshall’s 1931 Mohenjo-daro account (1922–1927); Mackay’s 1938 continuation (1927–1931); Vats’s 1940 Harappa report; Meadow’s 1991 edited Harappa 1986–1990 volume; and Rao’s 1979 Lothal ASI memoir [`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-mackay-1938`][`src-indus-vats-1940`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`][`src-indus-rao-lothal-1979`]. The Indus script remains without a generally accepted decipherment (no bilingual texts) — research status, not a claim of permanent undecipherability [`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`].

## Geography & environment

「大印度河区 / Greater Indus Valley」在 Kenoyer（1991）综述中包括印度河与加加尔–哈克拉（今干涸）平原与三角洲、东旁遮普与拉贾斯坦相关地带，以及西、北麓丘陵与山麓 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。Wright（2010）强调主要中心沿印度河与加加尔–哈克拉水系分布 [`src-indus-wright-2010`]。Kenoyer 指出两条水系水文特征不同：加加尔–哈克拉坡度较低、洪水相对不那么毁灭性，岸边与邻近平原保存遗址较多；印度河本身洪水更剧烈，相关遗址数量对比受此影响 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。平原大致以印度河五大支流汇合为单河处分为北（旁遮普）与南（信德）；北部夏冬降水相对较多，南部降雨更不稳定 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。海岸方向：印度河三角洲以西为莫克兰（Makran）干旱山地；以东为卡奇（Kutch）与索拉什特拉（Saurashtra），常与古吉拉特海岸平原并论但属不同亚区 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

Giosan 等（2012）以地貌与测年论证：全新世晚期干旱化约自 5000 BP 后使河流景观趋于稳定；旁遮普喜马拉雅大河停止强烈下切，信德印度河「巨型冲积脊」加积放缓；加加尔–哈克拉在全新世为**季风补给**河流，而非某些旧说主张的大型冰川补给喜马拉雅河（「失踪的娑罗室伐底」式冰川大河假说与宽深谷地缺失不符）[`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`]。作者将洪水强度逐渐下降与城市化/农业条件变化并置讨论——**气候—城市变迁是多因学术模型，不作单一决定论**（见 Notes）[`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`]。

“Greater Indus Valley,” in Kenoyer’s (1991) synthesis, includes the Indus and Ghaggar-Hakra plains and deltas, eastern Punjab and Rajasthan-related tracts, and western/northern piedmont zones [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Wright (2010) places major centers on the Indus and Ghaggar-Hakra systems [`src-indus-wright-2010`]. Kenoyer notes contrasting hydrology: the Ghaggar-Hakra’s lower gradient and less devastating floods correlate with more sites preserved along its banks; Indus flooding is more severe [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. A north (Punjab) / south (Sindh) division of the plain aligns with the confluence of five tributaries into a single Indus; northern rainfall is relatively higher, southern rainfall less predictable [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Coastal geography includes arid Makran west of the Indus delta and Kutch/Saurashtra to the east, often grouped with Gujarat but distinct as subregions [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

Giosan et al. (2012) argue late-Holocene aridification after ~5,000 BP stabilized fluvial landscapes; Punjab Himalayan rivers ceased strong incision while Sindh’s Indus mega-ridge aggraded more slowly; Holocene Ghaggar-Hakra rivers were **monsoon-fed**, not a large glacier-fed Himalayan river [`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`]. They link declining flood intensity to agricultural and settlement change — a **multi-causal scholarly model, not deterministic collapse** (Notes) [`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`].

Kenoyer 指出「Pre-Harappan / Early Harappan / Mature / Late」等旧标签定义重叠；Shaffer 的 Tradition–Era–Phase 框架被其采用为更连贯的总括 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。整合期开端约 2600 BCE 附近出现文字、砝码、哈拉帕式陶瓷等整合要素的密集共现；部分学者主张城市相可早至 2100–2000 BCE 结束，但哈拉帕新测年支持可延至约 1900 BCE [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

Kenoyer notes overlapping older labels; Shaffer’s Tradition–Era–Phase scheme is adopted as a coherent frame [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Integration around 2600 BCE marks dense co-occurrence of writing, weights, and Harappan ceramic styles; some scholars end the urban phase by 2100–2000 BCE, but Harappa dates support continuity to ~1900 BCE [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

整合期聚落常被通俗描述为严格南北—东西「棋盘格」街道与「西城堡 / 东下城」二分；Kenoyer 引 Jansen 等指出更贴切的是「不规则网状平面」，且高丘与低丘的配置并不总是西高东低的刻板二分 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。大型遗址常以多重土丘分区，反映城市分区；较小单丘遗址亦可有内部划分（如苏尔科塔达）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。聚落常建于或建在大规模泥砖墙/台基之上；哈拉帕部分墙段有烧砖护面；这类工程可兼防洪、抗侵蚀、界定城市行政空间与防御等功能——**「防御墙」不宜简化为唯一功能** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`]。

摩亨佐-达罗建筑再分析区分私人宅院（内向中庭、街门视线遮挡）、大型宅第外围附小单元复合体，以及开放通道式公共结构（大浴场、「粮仓」等）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。「大浴场 / Great Bath」「粮仓 / granary」等标签为推测性功能命名，精确用途不明 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`]。井与排水系统发达：Kenoyer 综述引 Jansen，按发掘区密度外推摩亨佐-达罗核心区井数或逾 700；井、沐浴平台以砖衬砌，小排水通至街沟与集污坑 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-mackay-1938`]。Wheeler（1968）等早期综合强调城堡/下城与「祭司—国王」图像传统——**属历史诠释层，须与现代发掘对照，不作本条制度定论** [`src-indus-wheeler-1968`][`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

政治形态：Kenoyer 综述呈多层聚落等级、标准化砝码与印章行政、跨区域物质文化同质性等，并讨论「国家」与「酋邦」等标签；其本人倾向视哈拉帕阶段为该区最早的国家级社会，但承认控制方式不同于两河/埃及的王陵—神庙包装 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。Possehl 等强调复杂性而未必具备两河式宫殿/神庙套件 [`src-indus-possehl-2002`]。**「帝国 / 单一领土帝国」vs「城市—区域网络」仍为开放争议——事实栏只描述可观察的城市与标准化证据，标签见 Notes** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]。

Popular descriptions of a rigid north–south / east–west grid and a fixed west-citadel / east-lower-town split oversimplify; Kenoyer, following Jansen, prefers an “irregular net plan,” with mound configurations more varied than a single bipolar template [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Large sites divide into multiple mounds; smaller single-mound sites may still be internally partitioned [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Massive mud-brick walls and platforms (sometimes with baked-brick facings at Harappa) can combine flood protection, erosion control, urban definition, and defense — **not “fortification only”** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`].

Mohenjo-daro architectural reanalysis distinguishes private courtyard houses, large houses with attached smaller units, and public structures with open access (Great Bath, so-called granaries) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Functional labels remain speculative [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`]. Wells and drains are elaborate; Kenoyer cites Jansen’s estimate of possibly >700 wells in Mohenjo-daro’s core from excavated densities [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-mackay-1938`]. Wheeler’s citadel / “priest-king” framings are historical interpretive layers, not adopted as institutional verdicts here [`src-indus-wheeler-1968`][`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

Political form: Kenoyer reviews multi-tier settlement, standardized weights and seals, and culture-wide material homogeneity, discussing “state” vs “chiefdom” labels; he favors state-level organization for the Harappan Phase while stressing differences from Mesopotamian/Egyptian palace–temple packages [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Possehl and others emphasize complexity without that package [`src-indus-possehl-2002`]. **“Empire” vs city–regional networks remains open debate — fact columns describe observable urbanism and standardization; labels stay in Notes** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`].

## Writing, language & administration

印度河文字（Indus script）是整合期区别于此前/此后阶段的关键要素之一：用于所有权/交易标识、记账、社会仪式记录及较随意的涂鸦等语境（综述取向）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`]。最常见载体是滑石阴刻印章（上缘短铭文 + 动物/复合神兽等图像），并有封泥、陶片刻画、釉砂/费昂斯小牌、工具与饰物上的铭文等 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`]。铭文通常很短（Kenoyer 综述：常约五个离散符号），且**缺乏双语对照文本**；五十余种「破译」主张无一被普遍接受 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`]。Parpola（1994）作为印章语料库主编，估计存世铭文样本近 4000 件（以印章与护符为主），并推进语标—音节 / 原始达罗毗荼语假说——**该破译方案不作为本条事实** [`src-indus-parpola-1994`]。书写方向多为自右向左；较长铭文可呈牛耕式交替 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

行政相关介质还包括**立方体石砝码**（Marshall 报告重量范围约 0.871–10,865 g；误差极低，指示严格控制）与容器/货捆封泥（洛塔尔仓库出土大量封泥，有时多印叠盖）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-rao-lothal-1979`]。语言归属（达罗毗荼 / 印度—雅利安 / 南亚语系等）在缺乏破译共识前**不能写成定论** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`]。

Indus writing is a hallmark of the Integration Era: ownership/transaction marking, accounting, socioritual recording, and graffiti contexts (survey orientation) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`]. Steatite intaglio seals (short inscription above animal/composite motifs) are the most common medium; impressions, pottery graffiti, faience tablets, and inscribed tools/ornaments also occur [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`]. Inscriptions are typically very short (~five discrete signs in Kenoyer’s survey) and **lack bilingual texts**; none of 50+ decipherment claims is generally accepted [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`]. Parpola (1994) estimates nearly 4,000 inscribed samples and advances a logo-syllabic / Proto-Dravidian hypothesis — **not adopted as fact here** [`src-indus-parpola-1994`]. Writing usually runs right to left; longer texts may be boustrophedon [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

Administrative media also include **cubical stone weights** (Marshall’s reported range ~0.871–10,865 g; very low error implies rigid control) and clay sealings on vessels/bales (Lothal warehouse assemblages; sometimes multiple seals) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-marshall-1931`][`src-indus-rao-lothal-1979`]. Language affiliation cannot be settled without accepted decipherment [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-parpola-1994`].

## Economy, subsistence & craft

生计具有区域多样性，不能用单一模式概括整个传统：农业与畜牧、渔猎采集互补；洪泛平原可在无大规模常设灌渠的情况下保持高产（与两河「巨型灌渠国家」叙事对照时尤须谨慎）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`]。传统类比下，冬季 *rabi* 作物（小麦、大麦、豆类、芝麻等）与季风/*kharif* 作物（棉花、黍稷、水稻等在古吉拉特等区）可并存；Kenoyer 综述强调至约公元前 2600 年多数地区已具备两季作物要素 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。卡利班甘发现犁耕田垄遗迹； Banawali 出土陶质玩具犁——支持犁耕与畜力牵引的物质线索 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。山麓有引水坝渠；阿富汗肖图盖（Shortugai）有灌渠证据；冲积平原上的古代灌渠识别仍困难 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。家畜包括瘤牛与非瘤牛、水牛、绵羊、山羊；城市期动物谱系更广，含淡水/海洋资源 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`]。

手工业高度分化：本地简单技术（木作、陶土、建筑）、非本地简单技术（石器）、本地复杂技术（精陶、石腕饰等）、非本地复杂技术（玛瑙珠、印章、铜/青铜、贵金属、海贝、釉砂）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。标准化砝码与部分精英制品常被解释为集中控制线索；陶器与饰物的「标准化」亦可来自共享审美与亲属学徒网络——**机制须分项讨论** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。原料与成品内部贸易证据包括海贝、玛瑙、天青石、铜等；外部接触见下节 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]。本条不给出未核对表格的人口、产量或贸易吨位数字。

Subsistence is regionally diverse: agriculture complemented by herding, hunting, and fishing; alluvial plains could be highly productive without massive permanent canal systems (caution vs Mesopotamian “hydraulic state” analogies) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`]. Traditional *rabi* (winter) and *kharif* (monsoon) crop suites could coexist; Kenoyer’s survey places elements of both across most regions by ~2600 BCE [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Kalibangan preserves a plowed-field grid; a toy terra-cotta plow from Banawali supports plow/traction farming [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Piedmont dams/canals and Shortugai irrigation are documented; identifying ancient canals on the alluvium remains difficult [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Domesticates include humped and non-humped cattle, water buffalo, sheep, and goat; urban faunal spectra broaden to freshwater/marine resources [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-meadow-harappa-1991`].

Crafts are highly differentiated across local/nonlocal materials and simple/complex technologies [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Standardized weights and some elite products are often read as centralized control; ceramic/ornament “standardization” may also reflect shared aesthetics and kin-based learning — **mechanisms must be itemized** [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Internal trade in shell, agate, lapis, copper, and finished goods is well attested; external contacts below [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]. No unchecked population, yield, or tonnage figures.

## External contacts

Kenoyer（1991）综述整合期与周边传统的联系：赫尔曼德传统与东南伊朗/中亚、阿拉伯湾海岸、半岛印度铜石并用文化，以及南部伊朗与两河城市文明 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。阿曼与海湾南岸出土哈拉帕式陶器、涂鸦乃至印章；两河遗址出土哈拉帕风格文物的年代跨度约公元前 2550–1300 年（经 Chakrabarti 等综述）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。楔形文字文献中的 **Meluhha（麦卢哈）** 地名，多数学者与印度河区对应——属文献—考古对接的学术共识取向，细节与波动仍须专文 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]。铜、锡、天青石等资源促使与俾路支—阿富汗等地建立贸易或殖民点（如肖图盖近天青石/铜锡源）[`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

关于外部贸易是否「造就」城市化：Kenoyer 综述认为海湾/两河贸易对哈拉帕城市政体的**起源**并非主因（内陆科特迪吉阶段网络与哈拉帕等中心先于或并行发展）；但城市确立后，外部贸易对维持内部控制结构仍关键 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。印度河—两河商品不对称（两河少见明确两河成品入印）提示间接经由海湾或南俾路支的可能 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]。与古埃及的宏观年代重叠不等于已证明的直接接触——本条不作埃及贸易定论 [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]。

Kenoyer (1991) surveys Integration Era links to the Helmand tradition and southeastern Iran/Central Asia, the Arabian Gulf, peninsular Chalcolithic cultures, and southern Iran/Mesopotamia [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Harappan-style pottery, graffiti, and seals appear in Oman and the southern Gulf; Mesopotamian contexts yield Harappan-style objects across a long span (via secondary compilations) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Cuneiform **Meluhha** is widely linked to the Indus region — a scholarly consensus orientation whose details still need dedicated entries [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-wright-2010`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]. Copper, tin, and lapis networks reach Baluchistan–Afghanistan (e.g. Shortugai) [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

On whether external trade “made” urbanism: Kenoyer argues Gulf/Mesopotamian trade was not the primary cause of Harappan urban origins (inland Kot Diji-phase networks and centers such as Harappa develop first), yet external trade remained critical to maintaining internal controls once cities existed [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`]. Asymmetry of finds (few clear Mesopotamian finished goods in the Indus) suggests indirect Gulf/southern Baluchistan routes [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`]. Chronological overlap with Egypt is not proof of direct contact [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].

## Notes

- **Script (research status):** Undeciphered Indus writing / lack of bilingual texts is recorded as **current research status**, not as permanent impossibility. Parpola’s Dravidian/logo-syllabic proposal and other decipherments remain contested and are **not** fact-column content [`src-indus-parpola-1994`][`src-indus-kenoyer-cambridge-2014`][`src-indus-wright-2010`].
- **Political form (open debate):** Whether the Integration Era is best labeled a territorial “empire,” a network of cities/polities, a “state,” or another form of complex society remains contested (Kenoyer reviews state vs chiefdom arguments; Possehl emphasizes complexity without a Mesopotamia-style palace/temple package; Ratnagar problematizes conventional state models). Keep empire/state labels in NOTES or `hypotheses/` [`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-possehl-2002`][`src-indus-ratnagar-2004`].
- **Ghaggar-Hakra / “Sarasvati”:** Giosan et al. 2012 reject a large Holocene glacier-fed Himalayan river across the Ghaggar-Hakra interfluve; Holocene channels there were monsoon-fed. Mythic-literary identifications are not archaeological proofs [`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`].
- **Lothal basin:** Rao’s “dockyard” identification is excavator interpretation; alternative reservoir readings exist in secondary debate — cite architecture, not settled function [`src-indus-rao-lothal-1979`][`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].
- **Climate and “collapse”:** Late aridification and fluvial quiescence are linked by Giosan et al. to agricultural vulnerability and eastward settlement shift; authors reject single-cause determinism. Prefer “transformation / Localization Era” over invasion-destruction narratives from older literature [`src-indus-giosan-pnas-2012`][`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`][`src-indus-wheeler-1968`].
- **ASI drafts:** Bisht Dholavira 2015 and Nath Rakhigarhi (1997–2000 seasons) are circulated drafts / website releases — cite as drafts, not sealed memoir numbers [`src-indus-bisht-dholavira-2015`][`src-indus-nath-rakhigarhi-draft`].
- **Rakhigarhi rank:** “Eastern capital / largest city” political-rank labels are hypotheses beyond what excavation reports alone settle [`src-indus-nath-rakhigarhi-draft`][`src-indus-kenoyer-1991`].
- **Kot Diji transition:** Conquest/conflagration-ending narratives between Kot Dijian and Mature levels are interpretive — not ethnic-replacement verdicts [`src-indus-khan-kot-diji-1965`][`src-indus-wheeler-1968`].
- **Ganweriwala:** Surface-survey site; size estimates are survey estimates; no invented trench inventory here [`src-indus-mughal-cholistan-1982`][`src-indus-mughal-cholistan-1997`].
- **Terminology:** “Indus Valley Civilization,” “Harappan Civilization,” and “Indus Tradition” overlap; this entry follows Kenoyer’s Tradition / Integration Era framing without declaring one name exclusive.
- **Chronology caution:** Marshall/Mackay/Vats absolute dates are historical; prefer Kenoyer 1991 / Wright 2010 / Giosan 2012 for ranges used here.
- No invented page numbers; quantitative survey/tablet/weight statistics require table-level follow-up before tighter figures.
