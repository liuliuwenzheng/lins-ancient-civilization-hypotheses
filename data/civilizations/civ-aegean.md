# 爱琴 / Aegean

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述爱琴青铜时代的学术常规框架：基克拉泽斯群岛、克里特（米诺斯）与希腊大陆（迈锡尼）的物质文化与社会史，而非某一宫殿的专论。Cynthia W. Shelmerdine 主编的《剑桥爱琴青铜时代指南》（2008）将范围写为约公元前 3000–1100 年的克里特、希腊与爱琴诸岛，并覆盖迈锡尼宫殿体系崩溃之后的时段；全书按年代与地理编排，便于比较不同文化 [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]。Oliver Dickinson（1994）将爱琴青铜时代框为大约公元前 3000–1000 年，并概述米诺斯与迈锡尼文明的兴衰，以及定居、经济、手工业、交换与近东接触、宗教与葬俗等主题 [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]。Cyprian Broodbank（2000）专著聚焦早期基克拉泽斯岛民考古，作为群岛侧的二手锚点（他也撰写 Shelmerdine 指南中的基克拉泽斯早期青铜时代专章） [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]。绝对年代方面，Manning 等（2006）用放射性碳与贝叶斯分析主张圣托里尼（Thera）喷发在公元前 17 世纪晚期，并使晚米诺斯 IA–II 相对「传统」考古年代拉长约百年——此为高年代方案的同行评议论证，**非**已闭合定论 [`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]。Arthur Evans 的《米诺斯宫》（1921–1935）是克诺索斯发掘的历史一手出版，须标注修复与叙事局限 [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`]。Carl Blegen 与 Marion Rawson（1966）报告麦西尼亚 Englianos 的「涅斯托尔宫」：1952–1964 季发掘的迈锡尼行政中心，出版说明称其在公元前 13 世纪末毁于火且未再占用 [`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`]。线性文字 B 经 Ventris & Chadwick（1956）《迈锡尼希腊语文书》确立为迈锡尼希腊语行政文书的标准读法参照 [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`]。线性文字 A（约公元前 1800–1450，主要见于克里特）在 Salgarella（2022）牛津古典词典条目中仍记为**未破译**——研究现况，非破译主张 [`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`]。

This seed summarizes the conventional scholarly frame for the Aegean Bronze Age across the Cyclades, Minoan Crete, and the Mycenaean mainland — not a single-palace monograph. Shelmerdine’s *Cambridge Companion* (2008) covers Crete, Greece, and the Aegean Islands from c. 3000–1100 BCE through the period after the Mycenaean palace system’s collapse [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]. Dickinson (1994) frames roughly 3000–1000 BC and the rise and fall of Minoan and Mycenaean civilizations, with thematic chapters on settlement, economy, crafts, exchange/Near Eastern contact, and religion/burial [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]. Broodbank (2000) anchors Early Cycladic island archaeology (he also wrote the Companion’s EBA Cyclades chapter) [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]. On absolute chronology, Manning et al. (2006) argue from ¹⁴C and Bayesian modeling that the Santorini (Thera) eruption falls in the late 17th century BCE and that LM IA–II stretch ~100 years versus conventional archaeological dates — a published “high chronology” case, **not** closed consensus [`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]. Evans’s *Palace of Minos* (1921–1935) is the historical primary Knossos publication, to be used with restoration/narrative caveats [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`]. Blegen & Rawson (1966) report the Palace of Nestor at Englianos (Messenia): University of Cincinnati campaigns 1952–1964; publisher summary states destruction by fire at the end of the 13th century B.C. and no reoccupation [`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`]. Ventris & Chadwick (1956) document Linear B as Mycenaean Greek administrative texts [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`]. Linear A (c. 1800–1450 BCE; mainly Crete) remains **undeciphered** per Salgarella’s 2022 OCD entry — research status, not a decipherment claim [`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`].
