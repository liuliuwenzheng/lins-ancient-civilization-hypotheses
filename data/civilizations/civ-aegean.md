# 爱琴 / Aegean

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

本条综述东地中海爱琴青铜时代的学术常规框架：基克拉泽斯群岛、克里特（米诺斯）与希腊大陆（迈锡尼）的物质文化与社会史，而非某一宫殿专论。Cynthia W. Shelmerdine 主编《剑桥爱琴青铜时代指南》（2008）将范围写为约公元前 3000–1100 年的克里特、希腊与爱琴诸岛，并覆盖迈锡尼宫殿体系崩溃之后的时段 [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]。Oliver Dickinson（1994）将爱琴青铜时代框为大约公元前 3000–1000 年，并概述米诺斯与迈锡尼文明的兴衰及定居、经济、手工业、交换与近东接触、宗教与葬俗等主题 [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]。Eric H. Cline 主编《牛津青铜时代爱琴手册》（2010）提供约公元前 3000–1000 年的专题与遗址级通论入口 [`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`]。Colin Renfrew（1972）《文明的出现》是第三千纪基克拉泽斯—爱琴文化序列与「乘数效应」过程论的里程碑综合 [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]。Cyprian Broodbank（2000）专著聚焦早期基克拉泽斯岛民考古 [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]。绝对年代方面，Warren & Hankey（1989）提供传统考古/埃及同步「低年代」手册框架；Manning 等（2006）用放射性碳与贝叶斯分析主张圣托里尼喷发在公元前 17 世纪晚期并使 LM IA–II 相对传统年代拉长约百年——**高/低年代仍是开放争议** [`src-aegean-warren-hankey-1989`][`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]。一手与历史发掘文献包括：Evans《米诺斯宫》（1921–1935，须标注修复局限）；Wace（1949）迈锡尼考古史与指南；Blegen & Rawson（1966）涅斯托尔宫；Doumas（1983）阿克罗蒂里发掘综述 [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]。线性文字 B 经 Ventris & Chadwick（1956）确立为迈锡尼希腊语行政文书；Chadwick（1976）据此重建迈锡尼世界；线性文字 A 在 Salgarella（2022）中仍记为**未破译** [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`][`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`]。

This entry summarizes the conventional scholarly frame for the Aegean Bronze Age across the Cyclades, Minoan Crete, and the Mycenaean mainland — not a single-palace monograph. Shelmerdine’s *Cambridge Companion* (2008) covers Crete, Greece, and the Aegean Islands from c. 3000–1100 BCE through the period after the Mycenaean palace system’s collapse [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]. Dickinson (1994) frames roughly 3000–1000 BC and thematic chapters on settlement, economy, crafts, exchange/Near Eastern contact, and religion/burial [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]. Cline’s *Oxford Handbook* (2010) orients periods, sites, and contacts across ca. 3000–1000 BC [`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`]. Renfrew (1972) is the landmark third-millennium culture-sequence and process synthesis [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]. Broodbank (2000) anchors Early Cycladic island archaeology [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]. Warren & Hankey (1989) supply a conventional archaeological/Egypt-synchronism chronology handbook; Manning et al. (2006) argue a late-17th-century BCE Thera eruption and stretched LM IA–II — **high vs low chronology remains open** [`src-aegean-warren-hankey-1989`][`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]. Excavation literature includes Evans’s *Palace of Minos* (restoration caveats), Wace’s Mycenae guide, Blegen & Rawson’s Palace of Nestor, and Doumas’s Akrotiri synthesis [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]. Ventris & Chadwick (1956) and Chadwick (1976) document Linear B as Mycenaean Greek administration; Linear A remains **undeciphered** [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`][`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`].

## Geography & environment

「爱琴」在此指爱琴海及其岛屿与沿岸陆地：基克拉泽斯群岛、克里特，以及希腊大陆南部与东部的青铜时代文化区 [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`]。Renfrew（1972）与 Broodbank（2000）强调岛屿环境、航海与第三千纪聚落格局对理解早期复杂化的重要性——岛屿并非大陆文明的附庸背景 [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`][`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]。克里特作为地中海最大岛屿之一，其宫室中心（克诺索斯等）与岛屿内部资源分区相关；大陆迈锡尼中心则与伯罗奔尼撒与中部希腊的河谷/防御地形相关 [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`]。圣托里尼（Thera）火山岛上的阿克罗蒂里遗址提供晚青铜时代城市型聚落被火山喷发埋藏的特殊保存条件 [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`][`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]。本条不在未核对表格前给出岛屿面积、航海距离或人口数字。

“Aegean” here means the Aegean Sea and its islands and littoral: the Cyclades, Crete, and southern/central Greek mainland Bronze Age culture areas [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`]. Renfrew (1972) and Broodbank (2000) stress island environments, seafaring, and third-millennium settlement patterning — islands are not a passive backdrop [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`][`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`]. Crete’s palatial centers and the mainland Mycenaean centers relate to island resource zoning and to Peloponnesian/central Greek valley and defensive topography [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`]. Akrotiri on Thera preserves an LBA urban settlement buried by volcanic eruption [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`][`src-aegean-manning-science-2006`]. No unchecked island-area, voyage-distance, or population figures are asserted.

## Key sites

- **克诺索斯 / Knossos** — 克里特关键宫室遗址；Evans《米诺斯宫》是历史一手出版，须标注修复与「米诺斯」叙事局限，并以现代综合交叉核对 [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]。
- **迈锡尼 / Mycenae** — 大陆迈锡尼文化名址；Wace（1949）提供发掘史与遗址指南取向 [`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]。

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

克里特「宫室」建筑复合体（多庭院、储藏、仪式与行政空间）是米诺斯考古的核心讨论对象；Evans 的克诺索斯重建与「米诺斯王宫」叙事影响深远，但现代综合强调须区分发掘记录、修复与解释 [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]。大陆迈锡尼宫殿中心（迈锡尼、皮洛斯等）以中央大厅（megaron）、 equ 防御工事与行政档案为特征；Chadwick 据 Linear B 讨论王权（wanax）、土地、配给与宗教职司等制度议题 [`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`][`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`]。阿克罗蒂里展示非「单一王宫」的密集城镇规划与壁画装饰房屋 [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]。Renfrew 从聚落层级与社会系统讨论第三千纪复杂化，而非把「文明」简化为宫殿出现 [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]。**「米诺斯海上霸权 / thalassocracy」** 作为解释模型有历史影响但受争议——见 Notes，不作定论 [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`]。

Cretan “palatial” complexes (courts, storage, ritual and administrative spaces) are central to Minoan archaeology; Evans’s Knossos restorations and “Palace of Minos” narrative remain influential but must be separated from excavation record and modern interpretation [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]. Mainland Mycenaean palatial centers feature megara, fortifications, and archives; Chadwick discusses wanax, land, rations, and cult offices from Linear B [`src-aegean-wace-mycenae-1949`][`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`][`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`]. Akrotiri shows dense town planning beyond a single palace model [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]. Renfrew approaches third-millennium complexity via settlement hierarchy and social systems [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]. **Minoan “thalassocracy”** is a historically influential but contested model — Notes, not settled fact [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`].

## Writing, language & administration

爱琴青铜时代书写传统主要包括：（1）**线性文字 A**（Linear A，约公元前 1800–1450，主要见于克里特）——Salgarella（2022）牛津古典词典条目记为**未破译** [`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`]；（2）**线性文字 B**（Linear B）——Ventris & Chadwick（1956）确立为记录迈锡尼希腊语的音节文字行政文书；Chadwick（1976）据此概述宫殿经济与社会 [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]。皮洛斯、克诺索斯等地的泥板档案是行政再分配、土地与人员登记的直接证据类型 [`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]。另有克里特象形文字等早期书写形式在手册中有专题入口；本条在未登记专书前不作破译或语属断言 [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`][`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`]。

Aegean Bronze Age writing traditions include: (1) **Linear A** (c. 1800–1450 BCE; mainly Crete) — **undeciphered** per Salgarella 2022 [`src-aegean-salgarella-linear-a-2022`]; (2) **Linear B** — Mycenaean Greek administrative syllabic script per Ventris & Chadwick 1956; Chadwick 1976 surveys palace economy and society from the tablets [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]. Archives at Pylos, Knossos, and other sites are direct evidence of administrative redistribution, land, and personnel records [`src-aegean-blegen-rawson-pylos-1966`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]. Cretan Hieroglyphic and other early scripts are oriented via handbooks; no decipherment or language-family claims without dedicated registered sources [`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`][`src-aegean-cline-oxford-handbook-2010`].

## Economy, subsistence & craft

Dickinson（1994）与 Shelmerdine 指南从定居农业、畜牧、手工业专业化与交换网络概述爱琴青铜时代经济 [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]。Linear B 文书显示谷物、油、羊毛、青铜与劳动力的宫殿记账传统 [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]。Renfrew（1972）讨论冶金、工艺专业化与贸易作为第三千纪「乘数效应」的组成部分 [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]。基克拉泽斯早期以航海、黑曜石与大理石等资源流通为岛民经济的重要侧面 [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`][`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]。阿克罗蒂里的房屋与壁画保存了城镇生活与手工艺的物质痕迹 [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]。本条不给出未核对表格的产量或人口数字。

Dickinson (1994) and Shelmerdine’s Companion survey Aegean Bronze Age economies via settled agriculture, herding, craft specialization, and exchange [`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`][`src-aegean-shelmerdine-companion-2008`]. Linear B documents palace bookkeeping for grain, oil, wool, bronze, and labor [`src-aegean-ventris-chadwick-1956`][`src-aegean-chadwick-1976`]. Renfrew (1972) treats metallurgy, craft specialization, and trade within a third-millennium “multiplier effect” [`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]. Early Cycladic seafaring and obsidian/marble circulation are island-economy themes [`src-aegean-broodbank-cyclades-2000`][`src-aegean-renfrew-1972`]. Akrotiri preserves urban household and craft traces [`src-aegean-doumas-thera-1983`]. No unchecked yield or population figures are asserted.

## Notes

- **Scope:** Early–Late Bronze Age Aegean regional overview (Cyclades / Minoan Crete / Mycenaean mainland). Not limited to Knossos or any one palace.
- **Linear scripts:** Linear B = Mycenaean Greek (Ventris & Chadwick 1956; Chadwick 1976). Linear A remains undeciphered (Salgarella 2022). Do not invent decipherments.
- **Absolute chronology (open debate):** “High” radiocarbon chronologies (e.g. Manning et al. 2006: Thera late 17th c. BCE) versus conventional archaeological / Egypt-synchronism (“low”) chronologies (Warren & Hankey 1989). Keep preferred calendar labels in Notes or `hypotheses/`.
- **Thalassocracy / Minoan sea power (open debate):** Evans-era and later models of a Minoan “thalassocracy” are historically influential but contested; not fact-library truth [`src-aegean-evans-palace-of-minos-1921`][`src-aegean-dickinson-1994`].
- **Evans caveats:** Restorations and political interpretations in *Palace of Minos* require modern cross-check.
- **Blegen destruction date:** “End of the 13th century B.C.” follows the 1966 publisher summary; later refinements may adjust phrasing.
- Dickinson ~3000–1000 BC, Shelmerdine c. 3000–1100 BCE, and Cline ca. 3000–1000 BC are overlapping conventional frames.
- **Density D-27:** structured pack expanded (sources 14→28; sites 4→14). Homeric Nestor=Englianos, single Knossos thalassocracy, Thera-sole-cause collapse, Sea Peoples→Pylos, Linear A=Greek, historic Agamemnon = hypotheses.
