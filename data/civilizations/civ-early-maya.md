# 早期玛雅 / Early Maya

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

本条概述玛雅文明的**早前古典期至早古典期**窗口，而非提卡尔、帕伦克、科潘等全盛古典城邦百科。Robert J. Sharer 与 Loa P. Traxler（2006）第 6 版《古代玛雅》将前哥伦布玛雅文明的长期演化框为约 2500 年——自中美洲复杂社会起源至 16 世纪西班牙征服；本条只用其作为长时段手册取向，**故意截断**在早古典期上沿 [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`]。Arthur Demarest（2004）剑桥通论与 Michael Coe & Stephen Houston（2015）第 9 版《玛雅》提供前古典繁荣与早期王朝的教学向综合 [`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]。Francisco Estrada-Belli（2011）聚焦古典期之前的仪式与权力：当提卡尔等处玛雅国王在公元 3 世纪雕刻第一批纪念碑、开启持续约六个世纪的古典期时，玛雅文明已近千年；纳克贝、埃尔米拉多尔等前古典城市拥有前西班牙美洲部分最大的神庙建筑，西瓦尔等遗址显示更早的复杂仪式 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]。Takeshi Inomata 等（2013）报告塞瓦尔发掘与 54 个放射性碳年代：约公元前 1000 年出现正式仪式空间，并在早中前古典期（约公元前 1000–700 年）发展为广场–金字塔 / E 组复合体；作者主张低地玛雅发展**不是**拉文塔的单向影响，而是区域互动 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]。Inomata 等（2019）进一步记录塞瓦尔前古典人造高原式大规模水平向工程 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`]。Richard Hansen（1998）综述纳克贝中前古典建筑发展；Clark & Hansen（2001）比较早期王权建筑与中形成期恰帕斯（MFC）空间模式 [`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]。Saturno、Stuart & Beltrán（2006）报告圣巴托洛晚前古典壁画与早期文字 [`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]。Harvard 皮博迪塞瓦尔多卷本（Willey 等）是该址古典期为主的历史一手系列；前古典再解释依赖 Inomata 项目 [`src-maya-willey-seibal-1975`]。Nichols & Pool（2012）与 Pool（2007）提供中美洲/海湾对照取向——非扩散定论 [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。

This entry covers the **Early Preclassic–Early Classic** window of Maya civilization — not an encyclopedia of high Classic cities. Sharer & Traxler (2006) frame roughly 2,500 years from origins to the Spanish Conquest; this entry uses that handbook only for long-view orientation and **stops** at Early Classic [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`]. Demarest (2004) and Coe & Houston (2015) supply Preclassic/early-dynasty survey orientation [`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]. Estrada-Belli (2011) focuses on ritual and power before the Classic: when Maya kings at Tikal dedicated first carved monuments in the 3rd century CE, Maya civilization was already nearly a millennium old; Nakbe and El Mirador raised some of the largest Prehispanic American temples; Cival shows earlier complex rituals [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]. Inomata et al. (2013) document Ceibal ceremonial space by ~1000 BCE growing into plaza-pyramid / E-Group architecture in 1000–700 BCE, arguing against one-way La Venta influence [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]. Inomata et al. (2019) document Preclassic artificial plateau construction at Ceibal [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`]. Hansen (1998) and Clark & Hansen (2001) orient Nakbe architecture and early kingship / MFC comparative models [`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]. Saturno, Stuart & Beltrán (2006) report Late Preclassic San Bartolo murals and early writing [`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]. Willey et al.’s Seibal Peabody series is historical primary for the site (Classic-focused); Preclassic reinterpretation relies on Inomata’s project [`src-maya-willey-seibal-1975`]. Nichols & Pool (2012) and Pool (2007) supply Mesoamerican/Gulf comparative context — not diffusion verdicts [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`].

## Geography & environment

早期玛雅讨论的地理核心是低地玛雅区：今危地马拉佩滕、伯利兹、墨西哥尤卡坦及相关地带的热带森林与季节性湿地景观；高地与太平洋沿岸在互动讨论中作为相邻区出现 [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]。塞瓦尔位于帕西翁河（Pasión）流域；米拉多尔盆地（含纳克贝、埃尔米拉多尔）位于中部低地内陆；圣巴托洛与西瓦尔等址分布在佩滕东北及相关地带 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]。Demarest 强调雨林环境对农业、聚落与政治组织的约束与可能性——本条不把环境决定论写成单一因果 [`src-maya-demarest-2004`]。Nichols & Pool 手册提供更广中美洲环境对照 [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]。

The geographic core for Early Maya discussion is the Maya lowlands — tropical forest and seasonal wetlands of Petén, Belize, Yucatán, and related zones; highlands and Pacific coast appear as interaction neighbors [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]. Ceibal lies on the Pasión drainage; the Mirador Basin (Nakbe, El Mirador) is inland in the central lowlands; San Bartolo and Cival occupy northeastern Petén and related zones [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]. Demarest stresses rainforest constraints and possibilities for agriculture and settlement — not a single-factor environmental determinism [`src-maya-demarest-2004`]. Nichols & Pool orient broader Mesoamerican environmental comparison [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`].

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

前古典低地出现正式广场、金字塔、E 组建筑组合与大规模土石工程。Inomata 等（2013）将塞瓦尔早中前古典广场–金字塔 / E 组置于低地已知最早之列，并与恰帕斯等地中形成期空间模式比较 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]。Inomata 等（2019）强调前古典**水平向人造高原**工程有时比高耸金字塔更耗费集体劳动，并与西瓦尔整平、米拉多尔 Danta 基底高原等比较——**比较为作者论述，体积细节须跟进专报** [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`]。纳克贝显示中前古典大型建筑发展；晚前古典米拉多尔盆地出现巨型神庙与三元组建筑，常被通论与日益集中的政治权威并置讨论——**「国家」标签仍属模型** [`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-demarest-2004`]。Clark & Hansen（2001）从早期王权宫廷建筑比较讨论制度起源——登记为比较模型 [`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]。Estrada-Belli 挑战「前古典仅为简单村落、被动响应高原」的旧模型 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]。

Preclassic lowlands see formal plazas, pyramids, E-Groups, and large earth-and-stone works. Inomata et al. (2013) place Ceibal’s early Middle Preclassic plaza-pyramid / E-Group among the earliest known lowland examples and compare Chiapas Formative spatial patterns [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]. Inomata et al. (2019) stress Preclassic **horizontal artificial plateaus** as major collective labor, comparing Cival leveling and El Mirador’s Danta basal plateau — **author comparisons; volumes need site reports** [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`]. Nakbe shows Middle Preclassic monumental growth; Late Preclassic Mirador Basin mega-temples and triadic architecture are often paired in surveys with emerging political centralization — **“state” remains a model label** [`src-maya-hansen-nakbe-1998`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-demarest-2004`]. Clark & Hansen (2001) compare early royal-court architecture — comparative model [`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`]. Estrada-Belli challenges older “simple Preclassic village” models [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`].

## Writing, language & administration

晚前古典低地已有文字实践：Saturno、Stuart & Beltrán（2006）报告圣巴托洛壁画建筑中的早期玛雅文字，碳十四语境指向约公元前 3 世纪——**早于**早古典王室石碑传统的全面展开 [`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]。古典期开端的雕刻王室纪念碑（约公元 3 世纪起）标志着长历法与王朝叙事在低地的制度化公开显示 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]。本条**不**展开古典期碑铭学语料库或完整王表；前古典行政是否已有宫殿式簿记，证据远弱于爱琴 Linear B——不以沉默论证「无行政」。

Late Preclassic lowlands already show writing practice: Saturno, Stuart & Beltrán (2006) report early Maya glyphs in San Bartolo’s painted architecture with ¹⁴C context in the 3rd century BCE — **earlier than** the full Early Classic stela tradition [`src-maya-saturno-stuart-beltran-2006`]. Early Classic carved royal monuments (from the 3rd century CE) mark public dynastic display [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]. This entry does **not** survey the Classic epigraphic corpus; Preclassic palace-style bookkeeping is far less attested than Aegean Linear B — silence is not proof of “no administration.”

## Economy, subsistence & craft

低地前古典–早古典生计建立在玉米等雨育/湿地农业、林地资源与区域交换之上；Demarest、Sharer & Traxler、Coe & Houston 提供雨林农业与手工业的通论取向 [`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]。大规模土石建筑本身暗示劳动动员与剩余生产组织——工程规模讨论见塞瓦尔高原与米拉多尔通论，**不作未核对的人口推算** [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]。玉器、黑曜石、陶器等工艺与礼仪经济在前古典中心可见；对象级类型学须跟进专报 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`][`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]。

Lowland Preclassic–Early Classic subsistence rests on maize and related rainforest/wetland farming, forest resources, and regional exchange; Demarest, Sharer & Traxler, and Coe & Houston orient the survey literature [`src-maya-demarest-2004`][`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`]. Monumental construction implies labor mobilization and surplus organization — discussed via Ceibal plateaus and Mirador surveys **without unchecked population estimates** [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-plateau-2019`][`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]. Jade, obsidian, and ceramics appear in Preclassic ritual economies; object typologies need site reports [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`][`src-maya-coe-houston-2015`][`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`].

## External contacts

与海湾奥尔梅克及恰帕斯、太平洋沿岸的形成期互动是早期低地玛雅起源辩论的核心。Inomata 等（2013）明确反对「拉文塔单向扩散 → 玛雅」作为定论，主张多区域互动；Clark & Hansen（2001）的 MFC / 早期王权比较提供恰帕斯侧模型入口 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。Estrada-Belli 批评「被动响应高原先进文化」的旧前古典模型 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]。`related_ids` 中的 `civ-olmec` 为年代与互动邻域交叉，**不是**母系谱系证明。

Interaction with Gulf Olmec, Chiapas, and the Pacific coast is central to early lowland Maya origins debates. Inomata et al. (2013) reject one-way La Venta diffusion as settled fact in favor of interregional interaction; Clark & Hansen (2001) supply MFC / early-kingship comparative models [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`][`src-maya-clark-hansen-2001`][`src-olmec-pool-2007`]. Estrada-Belli critiques older “passive highland stimulus” Preclassic models [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]. The `civ-olmec` cross-link is chronological/interaction neighborhood — **not** a mother-lineage proof.
