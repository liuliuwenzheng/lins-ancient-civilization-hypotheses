# 早期玛雅 / Early Maya

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述玛雅文明的**早前古典期至早古典期**窗口，而非提卡尔、帕伦克、科潘等全盛古典城邦百科。Robert J. Sharer 与 Loa P. Traxler（2006）第 6 版《古代玛雅》将前哥伦布玛雅文明的长期演化框为约 2500 年——自中美洲复杂社会起源至 16 世纪西班牙征服；本条只用其作为长时段手册取向，**故意截断**在早古典期上沿 [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`]。Francisco Estrada-Belli（2011）《第一玛雅文明》聚焦古典期之前的仪式与权力：当提卡尔等处玛雅国王在公元 3 世纪雕刻第一批纪念碑、开启持续约六个世纪的古典期时，玛雅文明已近千年；纳克贝（Nakbe）、埃尔米拉多尔（El Mirador）等前古典城市拥有前西班牙美洲部分最大的神庙建筑，西瓦尔（Cival）等遗址显示更早的复杂仪式；该书挑战「前古典仅为简单村落、被动响应高原先进文化」的旧模型 [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]。Takeshi Inomata 等（2013）在《科学》报告危地马拉塞瓦尔（Ceibal / Seibal）发掘与 54 个放射性碳年代的贝叶斯模型：约公元前 1000 年出现正式仪式空间，并在早中前古典期（约公元前 1000–700 年）发展为广场–金字塔 / E 组复合体，早于其他已知低地玛雅同类建筑，也早于奥尔梅克拉文塔作为主要中心的兴盛期；作者主张低地玛雅文明的发展**不是**拉文塔的单向影响，而是西南低地、恰帕斯、太平洋沿岸与南部海湾之间的区域互动 [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]。Nichols & Pool（2012）牛津手册提供中美洲区域通论取向 [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]。Pool（2007）将形成期早、中段叙述为玛雅等文明奠基背景——用作海湾侧对照，非扩散定论 [`src-olmec-pool-2007`]。

This seed covers the **Early Preclassic–Early Classic** window of Maya civilization — not an encyclopedia of high Classic cities. Sharer & Traxler’s 6th edition (2006) frames roughly 2,500 years from origins of complex society in Mesoamerica to the 16th-century Spanish Conquest; this entry uses that handbook only for long-view orientation and **stops** at Early Classic [`src-maya-sharer-traxler-2006`]. Estrada-Belli (2011) focuses on ritual and power before the Classic: when Maya kings at Tikal dedicated their first carved monuments in the 3rd century CE, inaugurating a Classic period of about six centuries, Maya civilization was already nearly a millennium old; Preclassic cities such as Nakbe and El Mirador raised some of the largest temples in Prehispanic America, and sites such as Cival show still earlier complex rituals; the book challenges older models of a simple Preclassic village age merely responding to highland stimuli [`src-maya-estrada-belli-2011`]. Inomata et al. (2013) report Ceibal excavations and Bayesian modeling of 54 radiocarbon dates: formal ceremonial space by ~1000 BCE grew into a plaza-pyramid / E-Group complex in the early Middle Preclassic (1000–700 BCE), earlier than other known lowland Maya examples and before La Venta’s rise as a major Olmec center; they argue lowland Maya development was not one-directional influence from La Venta but interregional interaction among the southwestern Maya lowlands, Chiapas, the Pacific Coast, and the southern Gulf Coast [`src-maya-inomata-ceibal-science-2013`]. Nichols & Pool (2012) orient the Mesoamerican handbook frame [`src-mesoamerica-nichols-pool-handbook-2012`]. Pool (2007) supplies Gulf-side Formative background for Maya foundations — comparative context, not a diffusion verdict [`src-olmec-pool-2007`].

## Notes

- **Scope:** Early Preclassic through Early Classic Maya (lowland ceremonial/urban beginnings; Preclassic kingdoms; onset of Classic royal monuments). **Not** a survey of Late Classic polities, epigraphy corpora, or Postclassic Yucatán.
- **Out of scope — Classic Maya “collapse”:** Demographic/political transformations at the end of the Classic are a major research field but **are not this entry’s focus**. Do not import collapse narratives here; open a separate sourced entry if needed.

Keep causal stories in NOTES or `hypotheses/`.
- **Not claimed:** that Estrada-Belli’s title implies the Maya were the world’s or Americas’ “first civilization.”
