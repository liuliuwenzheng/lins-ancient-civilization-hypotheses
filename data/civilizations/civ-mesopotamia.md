# 两河 / Mesopotamia

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

低地两河（Tigris–Euphrates）的早期城市与国家社会，是考古与历史综合研究的经典对象。Susan Pollock 的综合研究将该区早期国家/城市社会的主要时段大致框在约公元前 5000–2100 年 [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]。第四千纪的乌鲁克（Uruk）阶段，常被用作讨论南部两河早期城市化与复杂社会扩张的关键节点 [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]。J. N. Postgate 从社会与经济（考古与文本）角度概述早期两河制度与生计组织 [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。Harriet Crawford 主编的手册汇集苏美尔世界（Sumerian World）多专题学术章节，供进一步检索 [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]。大英博物馆与宾夕法尼亚大学博物馆联合远征队在乌尔（Ur）的发掘报告，记录了早于乌尔第三王朝（Ur III）的遗址层位与器物材料，属一手发掘文献 [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`]。

Lowland Mesopotamia is a classic setting for early urban and state societies. Pollock’s synthesis frames early state/urban developments over roughly the 5th–3rd millennia BCE [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]. The 4th-millennium Uruk period is a key horizon for southern Mesopotamian urbanization and related expansion [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]. Postgate surveys early Mesopotamian society and economy [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. Crawford’s edited handbook collects specialist chapters on the Sumerian world [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]. Woolley’s *Ur Excavations* IV is a primary report on pre–Ur III periods at Ur [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`].
