# 两河 / Mesopotamia

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

低地两河（Tigris–Euphrates）冲积平原上的早期城市与国家社会，是考古与历史综合研究的经典对象。Susan Pollock 的人类学综合将该区早期国家/城市社会的主要时段大致框在约公元前 5000–2100 年 [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]。Hans J. Nissen 从定居到早期领土国家给出长时段分期框架（约公元前 9000–2000 年）[`src-mesopotamia-nissen-1988`]。第四千纪乌鲁克（Uruk）阶段是讨论南部早期城市化、复杂行政与对外联系的关键节点 [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]。J. N. Postgate 从社会与经济（考古与文本）角度概述早期两河制度与生计组织 [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。D. T. Potts 从冲积环境与物质基础重建南部两河文明的基础设施 [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]。Robert McC. Adams 的区域地表调查综合，重建幼发拉底中部洪泛平原上的聚落与灌渠格局 [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]。Harriet Crawford 主编的《苏美尔世界》手册汇集多专题学术章节 [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]。Marc Van De Mieroop 的近东史通论为从早期城市/文字到更晚帝国的政治叙事提供教科书级框架（本条不展开其全书终点）[`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`]。

Lowland Mesopotamia is a classic setting for early urban and state societies. Pollock’s synthesis frames early state/urban developments over roughly the 5th–3rd millennia BCE [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]. Nissen supplies a long-run periodization from early settlement to first territorial states (ca. 9000–2000 BCE in his chapter scheme) [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-1988`]. The 4th-millennium Uruk period is a key horizon for southern urbanization, administration, and external linkage [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]. Postgate surveys early Mesopotamian society and economy [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. Potts rebuilds southern Mesopotamian civilization from environmental and material foundations [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]. Adams’s survey synthesis reconstructs settlement and irrigation patterning on the central Euphrates floodplain [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]. Crawford’s handbook collects specialist chapters on the Sumerian world [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]. Van De Mieroop’s Near Eastern history textbook orients political narrative from early cities/writing onward (this entry does not adopt his full terminal date) [`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`].

## Geography & environment

「美索不达米亚」字面指两河之间的土地；本条地理核心是今伊拉克南部为主的底格里斯–幼发拉底冲积低地，并向北延伸到上美索不达米亚（亚述地区）的相关讨论需另有出处支撑 [`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`][`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]。Potts 强调：南部冲积平原的农业、畜牧及相关产业（纺织、皮革、造船等）必须参照具体的河流水文与土壤条件来理解，而不能当作抽象「肥沃新月」口号 [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]。Adams 的地表调查工作表明，天然河道与人工灌渠的长期演变，与聚落兴衰、城市网络的空间格局紧密相关 [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]。Crawford 手册中的专题章节（含景观/水利类讨论）为进一步检索提供入口；本条不在未核对章节页码前摘录定量水利数据 [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]。

“Mesopotamia” denotes the land between the rivers; this entry’s geographic core is the Tigris–Euphrates alluvial lowland (especially southern Iraq), with northern Upper Mesopotamia noted only where separately sourced [`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`][`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]. Potts stresses that southern alluvial agriculture, animal husbandry, and related industries must be understood against specific river regimes and soil conditions [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`]. Adams shows that long-term evolution of natural watercourses and constructed canals is tightly linked to settlement histories and urban networks [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]. Specialist chapters in Crawford’s handbook (including landscape/hydraulic topics) are pointers for further reading; this entry does not excerpt quantitative canal metrics without checked page-level citation [`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`].

## Key sites

- **乌鲁克 / Uruk (Warka)** — 南部早期城市化与原始楔形文字行政的关键巨型遗址；德国考古队长期发掘，并有 AUWE 系列正式报告（含 Finkbeiner 对 1982–1984 年地表调查的 Endberichte）[`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`][`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]。Liverani 以乌鲁克期为南部城市与国家起源的历史分析对象 [`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]。
- **乌尔 / Ur** — 大英博物馆与宾夕法尼亚大学博物馆联合远征；Woolley《乌尔发掘》第 IV 卷报告乌尔第三王朝之前的遗址层位与器物 [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`]。
- **区域洪泛平原聚落系统** — Adams《城市心脏地带》基于幼发拉底中部洪泛平原地表调查，重建多千年聚落与土地利用格局（非单一「都城传记」）[`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]。
- **其他常在通论中出现的南部中心**（尼普尔 Nippur、拉格什/吉尔苏 Lagash/Girsu、基什 Kish、杰姆代特奈斯尔 Jemdet Nasr 等）——Roaf 地图集与 Crawford 手册提供地名与专题入口；本条在未登记各遗址发掘专报前，不编造具体「出土清单」[`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`][`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]。

- **Uruk (Warka)** — key southern mega-site for early urbanization and proto-cuneiform administration; long-running German excavations with AUWE final reports, including Finkbeiner’s 1982–1984 surface survey [`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`][`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`].
- **Ur** — Joint Expedition (British Museum + University of Pennsylvania Museum); Woolley *Ur Excavations* IV covers pre–Ur III periods and finds [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`].
- **Regional floodplain settlement systems** — Adams’s *Heartland of Cities* reconstructs multi-millennial settlement and land use from central Euphrates surveys (not a single-capital biography) [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`].
- **Other southern centers frequently named in surveys** (Nippur, Lagash/Girsu, Kish, Jemdet Nasr, etc.) — oriented via Roaf’s atlas and Crawford’s handbook; no invented find lists until site-specific reports are registered [`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`][`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`].

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

第四千纪南部出现以乌鲁克为代表的早期城市景观；Algaze 从「城市景观演化」角度讨论该期城市化与对外扩张 [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`]。Liverani 将乌鲁克期创新视为深刻改变社会文化诸层面的历史性转变，并讨论复杂经济的行政与早期国家政治文化（其「第一座城市」表述为作者论题标题，不作世界排名定论）[`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]。Postgate 综合早期两河的神庙、王权/宫殿与城乡经济关系等制度议题（考古证据与文本并读）[`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。Pollock 从早期国家/城市社会的人类学角度讨论权力、生产与意识形态的交织 [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]。Adams 则提醒：即便在「城市心脏地带」，城市权力相对农村腹地亦非永恒稳固，灌区农业的不确定性会反馈到聚落体系 [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]。纪念性建筑与神庙区的具体平面/地层，应以各遗址发掘报告为准（乌尔早期层位见 Woolley；乌鲁克调查见 Finkbeiner）[`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`][`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`]。

Fourth-millennium southern Mesopotamia sees an early urban landscape exemplified at Uruk; Algaze treats urbanization and related expansion as an evolving urban landscape [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`]. Liverani analyzes Uruk-period innovations as a deep societal transformation, including administration of a complex economy and early-state political culture (the “first city” phrase is his title framing, not a ranked verdict here) [`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]. Postgate synthesizes temples, palace/royal institutions, and urban–rural economic relations from archaeology and texts [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. Pollock discusses power, production, and ideology in early state/urban societies [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`]. Adams emphasizes that urban power over rural hinterlands was contingent; irrigation uncertainties feed back into settlement systems [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]. Monumental plans and temple stratigraphy must follow site reports (Ur early periods: Woolley; Uruk survey: Finkbeiner) [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`][`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`].

## Writing, language & administration

晚乌鲁克至杰姆代特奈斯尔阶段出现**原始楔形文字**（proto-cuneiform）行政记账传统：Nissen、Damerow 与 Englund 的《古代簿记》系统介绍早期书写与经济管理技术（数值系统、配给、商品账户等）[`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`]。CDLI（楔形文字数字图书馆计划）提供含原始楔形文字在内的文物目录与影像入口，具体泥板应引用其文物页/馆藏号，本条不编造 P 编号 [`src-mesopotamia-cdli`]。其后发展出更成熟的楔形文字体系，记录苏美尔语及后来的阿卡德语等；政治—行政史通论见 Van De Mieroop，社会—经济制度见 Postgate [`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。圆柱印章是行政封泥与身份/图像传统的重要介质，Collon 的大英博物馆综述介绍其在古代近东的序列与功能 [`src-mesopotamia-collon-1987`]。

Late Uruk to Jemdet Nasr contexts yield **proto-cuneiform** administrative bookkeeping; Nissen, Damerow, and Englund’s *Archaic Bookkeeping* presents early writing and economic administration (numerical systems, rations, commodity accounts) [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`]. CDLI provides catalog/image access to cuneiform artifacts including archaic texts; cite artifact pages/museum numbers for specific tablets — this entry invents no P-numbers [`src-mesopotamia-cdli`]. Later cuneiform records Sumerian and, subsequently, Akkadian among other languages; political-administrative narrative orientation: Van De Mieroop; society/economy: Postgate [`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. Cylinder seals are a major medium of administrative sealing and imagery; Collon’s British Museum survey outlines Near Eastern seal series and functions [`src-mesopotamia-collon-1987`].

## Economy, subsistence & craft

南部两河经济建立在冲积农业与水利管理之上，并与畜牧、纺织、皮革、造船等手工业相连 [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。Adams 的调查综合把聚落密度、灌渠与土地利用放在同一长期地理框架中讨论 [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]。原始楔形文字账板本身就是经济行政的直接证据类型（谷物、啤酒、配给等账户传统在《古代簿记》中概述）[`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`]。Postgate 强调将考古遗存与行政文本对照阅读，以理解早期两河的劳动组织与再分配 [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]。本条不在未核对表格前给出产量、人口或灌区面积的具体数字。

Southern Mesopotamian economies rest on alluvial agriculture and water management, linked to herding and crafts such as textiles, leather, and boat building [`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. Adams places settlement density, canals, and land use in one long-term geographic frame [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]. Proto-cuneiform accounts are themselves evidence of economic administration (grain, beer, rationing traditions as outlined in *Archaic Bookkeeping*) [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`]. Postgate stresses reading archaeological remains alongside administrative texts for labor organization and redistribution [`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`]. No yield, population, or irrigated-hectare figures are asserted here without checked tables.

第四千纪「乌鲁克扩张 / Uruk expansion」是讨论南部两河与周边地区物质文化联系的重要学术模型：Algaze 将其放在早期城市景观与区域互动框架中论述 [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`]。Liverani 亦讨论中心—边缘议题 [`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]。该模型在学界有支持与修订/批评；**本条将其标记为学术模型，而非已证实的单向殖民叙事**。与埃及、印度河等宏观区域的具体贸易路线或「殖民地」主张，须等待登记专门出处后再写入事实栏；目前 `related_ids` 仅为地理/年代交叉，不构成已证明的接触清单 [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`][`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`]。

The 4th-millennium **Uruk expansion** is a major scholarly model for southern Mesopotamian interaction with surrounding regions; Algaze frames it within early urban landscape and regional interaction [`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`]. Liverani likewise discusses center–periphery themes [`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`]. The model has support and revision/critique in the literature; **this entry labels it a scholarly model, not a proven one-way colonial narrative**. Specific trade-route or “colony” claims involving Egypt, the Indus, or other macro-regions require dedicated registered sources before entering fact columns; current `related_ids` are geographic/chronological cross-links only [`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`][`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`].

## Research history

现代两河考古的关键线索包括：乌尔的英—美联合发掘及其正式报告（Woolley）[`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`]；乌鲁克-瓦尔卡的德国长期工作与 AUWE 报告序列（含 Finkbeiner 调查卷）[`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`]；Adams 等对伊拉克南部的区域地表调查与聚落体系综合 [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]；柏林等地对乌鲁克古朴泥板的整理与 Englund 等对原始楔形文字的研究，并与 CDLI 数字目录衔接 [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`][`src-mesopotamia-cdli`]。综合层则有 Nissen、Pollock、Postgate、Potts、Algaze、Liverani、Van De Mieroop 与 Crawford 手册等 [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-1988`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`][`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`][`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`][`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`][`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]。Roaf 地图集为公众与教学向的地理定向 [`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`]。

Modern research pillars include the Joint Expedition to Ur and its final reports (Woolley) [`src-mesopotamia-woolley-ur-iv-1955`]; long-term German work at Uruk-Warka and the AUWE series (including Finkbeiner’s survey volume) [`src-mesopotamia-finkbeiner-uruk-auwe4-1991`]; Adams’s regional surface surveys and settlement synthesis [`src-mesopotamia-adams-1981`]; archaic Uruk tablet projects linked to Englund and to CDLI [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-damerow-englund-1993`][`src-mesopotamia-cdli`]. Syntheses include Nissen, Pollock, Postgate, Potts, Algaze, Liverani, Van De Mieroop, and Crawford’s handbook [`src-mesopotamia-nissen-1988`][`src-mesopotamia-pollock-1999`][`src-mesopotamia-postgate-1992`][`src-mesopotamia-potts-1997`][`src-mesopotamia-algaze-2008`][`src-mesopotamia-liverani-uruk-2006`][`src-mesopotamia-van-de-mieroop-2015`][`src-mesopotamia-crawford-sumerian-world-2013`]. Roaf’s atlas remains a public/teaching geographic orientation [`src-mesopotamia-roaf-1990`].
