## Claims (EN)

- (fact) Literature frames Pueblo Bonito as one of Chaco Canyon's hallmark great houses (UNESCO 353; Vivian 1990).
- (fact) UNESCO frames Chaco Culture and Mesa Verde as representative landscapes of Ancestral Puebloan architecture and settlement in the U.S. Southwest (UNESCO 353, 27).
- (fact) Cliff Palace is discussed by NPS/UNESCO as a representative Mesa Verde cliff dwelling (UNESCO 27; NPS).
- (fact) Lekson's synthesis places ancient Southwest political centres including Chaco nodes in a long-term narrative (Lekson 2009).
- (fact) Aztec Ruins and Salmon among other Chacoan outlier great houses are discussed in regional-system literature (Vivian 1990; Lekson).
- (fact) Pecos Classification labels (Basketmaker, Pueblo II/III, etc.) structure Southwest chronology working frames (Plog 2008); absolute dates remain revisable.
- (fact) Plog 2008 provides an accessible synthesis of ancient peoples of the American Southwest (Thames and Hudson).
- (fact) Sebastian 1992 discusses sociopolitical evolution models for Chaco Anasazi (CUP); models and ethnonyms remain revisable.
- (fact) UNESCO lists Chaco Culture as World Heritage, emphasizing great houses, roads, and ceremonial landscape (UNESCO 353).
- (fact) UNESCO lists Mesa Verde National Park as World Heritage, emphasizing cliff dwellings and mesa settlements (UNESCO 27).
- (fact) Vivian 1990 establishes a Chacoan prehistory synthesis for the San Juan Basin (Academic Press).
