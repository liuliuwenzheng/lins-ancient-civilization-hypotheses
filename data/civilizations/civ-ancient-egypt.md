# 古埃及 / Ancient Egypt

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述尼罗河流域前王朝至早王朝阶段的学术常规框架。Ian Shaw 主编的牛津通史将 Naqada 时期列为约公元前 4000–3200 年（Midant-Reynes 章），并将埃及国家的出现列为约公元前 3200–2686 年（Bard 章）[`src-egypt-shaw-oxford-history-2000`]。Béatrix Midant-Reynes 的专著系统讨论从史前到早期法老门槛的埃及前王朝考古 [`src-egypt-midant-reynes-prehistory-2000`]。Toby Wilkinson 的专著聚焦早王朝埃及——金字塔时代之前数百年的形成期制度与考古 [`src-egypt-wilkinson-early-dynastic-1999`]。

This seed summarizes the conventional scholarly frame for Predynastic to Early Dynastic Egypt. Shaw’s Oxford handbook lists the Naqada period as c. 4000–3200 BC (Midant-Reynes) and the emergence of the Egyptian state as c. 3200–2686 BC (Bard) [`src-egypt-shaw-oxford-history-2000`]. Midant-Reynes’s monograph covers Egyptian prehistory through the threshold of the early state [`src-egypt-midant-reynes-prehistory-2000`]. Wilkinson’s monograph treats Early Dynastic Egypt as the formative centuries of Pharaonic civilization before the pyramid age [`src-egypt-wilkinson-early-dynastic-1999`].

## Notes

- **State formation / “unification”:** Popular accounts sometimes treat the Narmer Palette as proof of a single military unification. This library does **not** record that as settled fact. Prefer Bard’s “emergence of the Egyptian state” framing in Shaw and Wilkinson’s Early Dynastic synthesis: a longer process, with iconography as evidence to be interpreted, not a one-event proof [`src-egypt-shaw-oxford-history-2000`][`src-egypt-wilkinson-early-dynastic-1999`].
