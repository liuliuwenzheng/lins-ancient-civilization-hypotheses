# 安第斯早期文明（卡拉尔–北奇科） / Andean early civilizations (Caral–Norte Chico)

> **Status:** fact-library — factual summary only. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.
> **Depth:** follows [`docs/entry-depth.md`](../../docs/entry-depth.md). Every major section cites registered `src-*` ids.

## Summary

秘鲁中北部沿海 Norte Chico（华乌拉 Huaura、苏佩 Supe、帕蒂维尔卡 Pativilca、福塔莱萨 Fortaleza 四河谷）在晚古风期出现一批以大型纪念性建筑、广泛居住建筑、且缺乏陶器为共性的大型遗址群。Haas、Creamer 与 Ruiz（2004）发表 95 个新的放射性碳年代（取样于该区 20 余处大型早期遗址中的 13 处），将这些遗址的兴起与发展框在约公元前 3000–1800 校准年（cal BCE）[`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]。苏佩河谷的卡拉尔（Caral）：Shady、Haas 与 Creamer（2001）的放射性碳工作将纪念性公共建筑、城市聚落与灌溉农业相关年代框在约公元前 2627–1977 校准年；遗址距太平洋约 23 公里，中心纪念性/居住/非居住建筑区约 65 公顷，并为苏佩河谷约 18 处大型前陶遗址之一 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。Creamer 等对帕蒂维尔卡（2007）与福塔莱萨（2013）河谷的系统试掘与测年，把同一晚古风窗口扩展到邻谷纪念性遗址（含沉陷圆形广场、阶梯平台丘等）[`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。Haas 等（2013）进一步报告北美奇科内陆遗址的玉米花粉、石器残渣与粪便淀粉证据 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]。Shady 1997 与 Shady & Leyva 2003 登记卡拉尔–苏佩项目早期专著与编辑文集脉络；本条不从中抽取未核验页码主张 [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]。海岸前陶复合体与「海洋生计基础」模型的经典讨论见 Moseley、Raymond 等；其解释地位见 Notes [`src-andes-moseley-maritime-1975`][`src-andes-raymond-maritime-reconsideration-1981`][`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`]。

Norte Chico on Peru’s north-central coast (Huaura, Supe, Pativilca, Fortaleza valleys) hosts a Late Archaic cluster of large sites sharing monumental architecture, extensive residential architecture, and lack of ceramics. Haas, Creamer & Ruiz (2004) report 95 new radiocarbon dates from 13 of more than 20 large early sites, framing the complex between ~3000 and 1800 cal BCE [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]. At Caral in the Supe Valley, Shady, Haas & Creamer (2001) place monumental corporate architecture, urban settlement, and irrigation agriculture between ~2627 and 1977 cal BCE; the site lies ~23 km inland, with a ~65 ha central architectural zone, and is one of 18 large preceramic Supe Valley sites [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Creamer and colleagues’ systematic testing in the Pativilca (2007) and Fortaleza (2013) valleys extends the same Late Archaic window to neighboring monumental sites (including sunken circular plazas and stepped platform mounds) [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]. Haas et al. (2013) report maize pollen, stone-tool residues, and coprolite starch evidence from inland Norte Chico contexts [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]. Shady (1997) and Shady & Leyva (2003) document Caral-Supe project literature; no unverified page-level claims are taken from those volumes here [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]. Classic debates on coastal Preceramic complexity and “maritime foundations” are oriented via Moseley, Raymond, and Aspero studies — interpretive status in Notes [`src-andes-moseley-maritime-1975`][`src-andes-raymond-maritime-reconsideration-1981`][`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`].

## Geography & environment

Norte Chico 位于利马以北的秘鲁中北部太平洋沿岸，由四条相邻河谷组成：华乌拉、苏佩、帕蒂维尔卡与福塔莱萨 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]。该区遗址既有近海者（如苏佩河口一带的阿斯佩罗 Aspero），亦有深入内陆十余至二十余公里者（如卡拉尔距海约 23 km；福塔莱萨谷的瓦里坎加 Huaricanga 约 23 km 内陆；卡瓦列特 Caballete 约 8 km 内陆）[`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`][`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`][`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`]。Science 2001 将卡拉尔的纪念性建筑与**灌溉农业**并置讨论，提示干旱海岸河谷农业依赖河谷水资源管理 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。Fieldiana 福塔莱萨报告描述河谷下游冲积平原与山麓低丘地带为调查范围，并指出部分河道季节性干涸等水文特征（细节以报告本文为准；本条不摘录未核对的流量数字）[`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。更南的奇尔卡（Chilca）谷帕洛马（Paloma）前陶村落为**比较性**海岸前陶景观，地理上不属于 Norte Chico 核心四谷 [`src-andes-quilter-paloma-1989`]。

Norte Chico lies on Peru’s north-central Pacific coast north of Lima and comprises four adjacent valleys: Huaura, Supe, Pativilca, and Fortaleza [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]. Sites range from near-shore locations (e.g. Aspero near the Supe mouth) to inland settlements tens of kilometers from the sea (Caral ~23 km inland; Huaricanga ~23 km; Caballete ~8 km) [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`][`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`][`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`]. Science 2001 pairs Caral’s monumental architecture with **irrigation agriculture**, pointing to water management in arid coastal valleys [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. The Fortaleza Fieldiana report situates survey in the lower-valley floodplain and foothill zone and notes seasonal hydrology (no unchecked discharge figures excerpted here) [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]. Paloma in the Chilca Valley is a **comparative** coastal Preceramic landscape south of Norte Chico’s four-valley core [`src-andes-quilter-paloma-1989`].

## Key sites

- **卡拉尔 / Caral (Caral-Supe，旧称 Chupacigarro Grande 等)** — 苏佩河谷关键大型前陶遗址；中心建筑区约 65 ha；距海约 23 km；纪念性公共建筑与灌溉农业年代约 2627–1977 cal BCE；为苏佩谷约 18 处大型前陶遗址之一 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。项目早期概述与编辑文集：Shady 1997；Shady & Leyva 2003 [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]。2009 年以「Sacred City of Caral-Supe」列入教科文世界遗产名录（**遗产命名 ≠ 考古定论**）[`src-andes-unesco-caral-2009`]。
- **阿斯佩罗 / Aspero** — 苏佩河口海岸大型前陶遗址；Moseley & Willey（1973）重新识别人工平台丘 / 「公司性劳动」建筑；Feldman（1987）从建筑论证海岸前陶非平等社会倾向 [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`]。PNAS 2013 回顾该址曾出土玉米穗轴丛，但早期层位与测年语境曾不确定 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]。
- **卡瓦列特 / Caballete（福塔莱萨谷）** — 约 8 km 内陆；中央建筑复合体以六座大型平台丘大致呈「U」形环绕开敞广场；居住区与较小建筑环丘分布；晚古风试掘与测年见 Fieldiana 2013，饮食/玉米证据见 PNAS 2013 [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`][`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]。
- **瓦里坎加 / Huaricanga（福塔莱萨谷）** — 约 23 km 内陆；以一座特大型丘为主、两侧较小丘；居住与仪式房间试掘见 PNAS/Fieldiana [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。
- **福塔莱萨谷其他试掘点** — Porvenir、Cerro Blanco 1–2、Shaura 等（2003–2004 试掘；80 个碳十四样本）[`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。
- **帕蒂维尔卡谷试掘点** — 九处遗址系统试测；七处晚古风占用约 2740–1870 cal BCE；Potao 样指示后续 Initial Period [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`]。
- **苏佩谷其他前陶大址（名录取向）** — Science 2001 提及邻址更名：Lurihuasi、Miraya、Chupacigarro 等；细节与发掘程度以项目文献为准，本条不编造出土清单 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]。
- **帕洛马 / Paloma（比较址，非 Norte Chico 核心）** — 奇尔卡谷前陶村落与墓葬研究 [`src-andes-quilter-paloma-1989`]。

- **Caral (Caral-Supe)** — keystone large preceramic Supe Valley site; ~65 ha central zone; ~23 km inland; monumental architecture / irrigation agriculture ~2627–1977 cal BCE; one of ~18 large Supe preceramic sites [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Project literature: Shady 1997; Shady & Leyva 2003 [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]. UNESCO inscription 2009 under “Sacred City of Caral-Supe” (**heritage title ≠ archaeological verdict**) [`src-andes-unesco-caral-2009`].
- **Aspero** — large coastal preceramic site at the Supe mouth; Moseley & Willey (1973) recognized artificial platform mounds / corporate labor structures; Feldman (1987) argues architectural evidence for nonegalitarian coastal Preceramic systems [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`].
- **Caballete (Fortaleza)** — ~8 km inland; six large platform mounds in a rough U around a plaza; Late Archaic testing and maize/diet evidence [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`][`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`].
- **Huaricanga (Fortaleza)** — ~23 km inland; one very large mound with smaller flanking mounds [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`].
- **Other Fortaleza tested sites** — Porvenir, Cerro Blanco 1–2, Shaura [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`].
- **Pativilca tested sites** — nine sites tested; seven Late Archaic occupations ~2740–1870 cal BCE [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`].
- **Other Supe preceramic centers (name orientation)** — Lurihuasi, Miraya, Chupacigarro, etc., per Science 2001 renaming note; no invented find lists [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`].
- **Paloma (comparative, outside Norte Chico core)** — Chilca Valley Preceramic village/mortuary study [`src-andes-quilter-paloma-1989`].

## Urbanism, architecture & institutions

晚古风 Norte Chico 遗址群的共性包括：大规模纪念性建筑、广泛居住建筑、无陶 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]。Science 2001 将卡拉尔的纪念性「公司性 / corporate」建筑与「城市聚落 / urban settlement」表述并置——**「城市」在此为论文用语，是否等于旧大陆城市定义，见 Notes 开放争议** [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。福塔莱萨 Fieldiana 报告确认阶梯金字塔式平台丘、沉陷圆形广场（sunken circular plazas）、竖立独石（huancas）等纪念性要素；平台丘与沉陷圆形广场配对的模式至少可追溯到公元前第三千纪；卡瓦列特、波韦尼尔等处可见 U 形布局倾向 [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。纪念性建筑常用 **shicra**（纤维网袋装填河卵石/石块）作为填充/建造技术，并因此保存可用于测年的植物纤维 [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。阿斯佩罗平台丘被 Moseley & Willey、Feldman 解读为需要动员劳动力的「公司性」工程，并据此讨论社会不平等萌芽——**属学术模型** [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`]。Shady & Leyva 2003 书名使用「ciudad sagrada / Estado prístino」等表述，登记为项目诠释传统，**不写入本条事实定论栏** [`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]。Haas & Creamer（2004）在 Silverman 手册中提供中安第斯晚古风文化转型的二手综合框架 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-silverman-2004`]。

Shared Norte Chico Late Archaic traits include large-scale monumental architecture, extensive residential architecture, and aceramic assemblages [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]. Science 2001 pairs Caral’s monumental corporate architecture with “urban settlement” — **whether “city” matches Old World urban definitions remains an open debate (Notes)** [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Fortaleza Fieldiana documents stepped platform mounds, sunken circular plazas, and upright monoliths (*huancas*); mound–plaza pairing is present by the third millennium BCE; U-shaped layouts appear at sites such as Caballete and Porvenir [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]. Monumental fills often use **shicra** (fiber bags of cobbles), which also yield datable plant fiber [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]. Aspero platforms are read by Moseley & Willey and Feldman as corporate labor works linked to emerging inequality — **scholarly models** [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`]. “Ciudad sagrada” / “Estado prístino” language in Shady & Leyva 2003 is project interpretive framing, **not adopted here as settled fact** [`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]. Haas & Creamer (2004) supply a handbook synthesis of Central Andean Late Archaic transformations [`src-andes-haas-creamer-silverman-2004`].

## Writing, language & administration

已登记的 Norte Chico 晚古风测年与试掘文献强调**缺乏陶器**，并报告建筑、居住垃圾、植物遗存与碳十四样本；**未报道文字系统或行政泥板传统** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`][`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。若存在非文字的组织/仪式沟通媒介（纺织、建筑布局、音乐器具等），须等待登记专门出土报告后再写入；本条不臆测「无文字即无行政」。

Registered Norte Chico Late Archaic dating and testing literature emphasizes **lack of ceramics** and reports architecture, residential refuse, botanical remains, and radiocarbon samples; **no writing system or tablet administration is reported** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-haas-pativilca-2007`][`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`][`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Non-script media of organization/ritual (textiles, architectural layout, instruments, etc.) require dedicated registered reports before entering fact columns; this entry does not equate “no writing” with “no administration.”

## Economy, subsistence & craft

Science 2001 将卡拉尔与**灌溉农业**并置，作为美洲早期纪念性建筑语境中的生计要素 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。Haas 等（2013）在帕蒂维尔卡与福塔莱萨内陆遗址报告玉米（*Zea mays*）花粉、石器淀粉/植硅体残渣与人/犬粪便中的玉米淀粉；作者认为玉米在约公元前 3000–1800 年被广泛种植、加工并构成饮食重要组成部分——**「主粮」程度仍属作者解释，学界玉米重要性长期有讨论** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]。同文还提到甘薯（*Ipomoea batatas*）淀粉、番石榴作为糖分来源、以及凤尾鱼等海洋蛋白出现在粪便证据中，提示**河谷农业与海岸资源并读** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]。Moseley（1975）提出早期中海岸复杂化可主要建立在海洋资源、定居、棉花/网具技术与公司性纪念建筑之上（「海洋生计基础」模型）；Raymond（1981）从热量估算、农业潜力、保存偏差与遗址分布提出系统质疑 [`src-andes-moseley-maritime-1975`][`src-andes-raymond-maritime-reconsideration-1981`]。阿斯佩罗作为海岸前陶大址，是该辩论的经典田野锚点 [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`]。建造技术上的 shicra 纤维袋属可观察工艺传统 [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]。本条不给出未核对表格的人口、产量或渔获量数字。

Science 2001 links Caral to **irrigation agriculture** alongside monumental architecture [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Haas et al. (2013) report maize pollen, tool residues, and coprolite starches at inland Pativilca/Fortaleza sites; they argue maize was widely grown, processed, and dietarily important ca. 3000–1800 BCE — **“staple” intensity remains the authors’ interpretation within a long maize debate** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]. The same study notes sweet-potato starch, guava as a sugar source, and anchovies as protein in coprolites, implying **valley farming read together with marine resources** [`src-andes-haas-creamer-pnas-maize-2013`]. Moseley’s (1975) maritime-foundations model and Raymond’s (1981) reconsideration frame the classic coastal subsistence debate [`src-andes-moseley-maritime-1975`][`src-andes-raymond-maritime-reconsideration-1981`]. Aspero anchors that debate archaeologically [`src-andes-moseley-willey-aspero-1973`][`src-andes-feldman-andean-state-1987`]. Shicra bag construction is an observable craft/building tradition [`src-andes-creamer-ruiz-perales-haas-fortaleza-2013`]. No unchecked population, yield, or catch statistics are asserted.

## Notes

- **Scope:** Norte Chico Late Archaic (Caral–Supe and neighboring valleys). **Not** a survey of Chavín, Moche, Wari, Tiwanaku, Inca, or other later Andean traditions (oriented only via Burger / research history).
- **Open scholarly debate — city vs ceremonial center:** Science 2001 and project titles use “urban settlement” / “ciudad sagrada”; whether sites meet cross-cultural “city” criteria (residential density, economic differentiation, etc.) remains debated. This entry records monumental + residential architecture as evidenced, without ranking “first city in the Americas.”
- **Open scholarly debate — state vs complex society / polity:** Shady & Leyva 2003 “Estado prístino” language and secondary “civilization” marketing are **not** treated as settled political typology here [`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`][`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`].
