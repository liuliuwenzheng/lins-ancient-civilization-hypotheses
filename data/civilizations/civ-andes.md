# 安第斯早期文明（卡拉尔–北奇科） / Andean early civilizations (Caral–Norte Chico)

> **Status:** library-seed — cited factual overview. Hypotheses belong in `hypotheses/`.

## Summary

本条概述秘鲁中北部沿海 Norte Chico（华乌拉 Huaura、苏佩 Supe、帕蒂维尔卡 Pativilca、福塔莱萨 Fortaleza 四河谷）晚古风期的早期纪念性与居住建筑复合体。Haas、Creamer 与 Ruiz（2004）发表 95 个新的放射性碳年代，取样于该区 20 余处大型早期遗址中的 13 处，将这些遗址的兴起与发展框在约公元前 3000–1800 校准年（cal BCE）；遗址群具有大规模纪念性建筑、广泛居住建筑、且缺乏陶器等共性 [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]。苏佩河谷的卡拉尔（Caral）遗址：Shady、Haas 与 Creamer（2001）的放射性碳工作将纪念性公共建筑、城市聚落与灌溉农业相关年代框在约公元前 2627–1977 校准年；遗址距太平洋约 23 公里，中心纪念性/居住/非居住建筑区约 65 公顷，并为苏佩河谷约 18 处大型前陶遗址之一 [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]。Shady 1997 与 Shady & Leyva 2003 为卡拉尔–苏佩项目早期专著与编辑文集，登记遗址研究脉络；本条不从中抽取未核验页码主张 [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`]。

This seed summarizes Late Archaic early monumental and residential complexes in coastal Peru’s Norte Chico (Huaura, Supe, Pativilca, and Fortaleza valleys). Haas, Creamer & Ruiz (2004) report 95 new radiocarbon dates from 13 of more than 20 large early sites, framing the complex between ~3000 and 1800 cal BCE; shared traits include large-scale monumental architecture, extensive residential architecture, and lack of ceramics [`src-andes-haas-creamer-ruiz-norte-chico-2004`]. At Caral in the Supe Valley, Shady, Haas & Creamer (2001) place monumental corporate architecture, urban settlement, and irrigation agriculture between ~2627 and 1977 cal BCE; the site lies ~23 km inland, with a ~65 ha central architectural zone, and is one of 18 large preceramic Supe Valley sites [`src-andes-shady-haas-creamer-caral-2001`]. Shady (1997) and Shady & Leyva (2003) document the Caral-Supe project literature; no unverified page-level claims are taken from those volumes here [`src-andes-shady-caral-1997`][`src-andes-shady-leyva-caral-supe-2003`].
